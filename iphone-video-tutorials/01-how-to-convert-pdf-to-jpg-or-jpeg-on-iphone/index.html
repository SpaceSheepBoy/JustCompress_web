How to Convert PDF to JPG or JPEG on iPhone

Turn PDF pages into photo files when you need to upload, share, or save pages as images.

Watch on YouTube

Quick steps

This is for iPhone users who want to finish the job without learning a big app. The video shows the full flow, and these steps keep it simple.

  1. Open Just PDF Compressor on your iPhone.
  2. Choose the PDF you want to turn into images.
  3. Select the PDF to JPG or JPEG option.
  4. Export the pages as photo files and save or share them.

What app do I need?

Use Just PDF Compressor. You can open the App Store link, or search Just PDF Compressor in the App Store.
