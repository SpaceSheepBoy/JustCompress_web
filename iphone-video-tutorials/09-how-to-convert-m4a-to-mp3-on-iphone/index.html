How to convert M4A to MP3 on iPhone

Make an MP3 copy when the receiving person or upload form specifically needs MP3. Keep your original M4A recording.

Quick answer: Open Convert in Audio Compressor MP3 Converter, choose your M4A file, select MP3 as the output format, choose quality and convert. Save the result to Files and play the saved copy before sending it. Renaming a file from .m4a to .mp3 does not convert its audio.

Conversion steps · Recorded walkthrough · Check the result

Create the MP3 copy

  1. Prepare a playable source. Find the M4A in Files and check that it plays. For a Voice Memos recording, use its share options to save an audio copy to Files first. Wait for an iCloud file to finish downloading before selecting it.
  2. Choose Convert. Open Audio Compressor MP3 Converter, choose Convert and select that M4A file. Use the conversion workflow when you need a different output format.
  3. Select MP3 and quality. In Output Format, choose MP3. Quality affects the size and sound of the encoded copy. Start with a setting suited to the recording, then listen to the result; the smallest preset may lose detail you need.
  4. Convert and save. Start conversion and wait for the result. Tap Save audio, choose a folder in Files and keep the original M4A separately.
  5. Check the saved file. Open the new MP3 and listen to its beginning, middle and end. Confirm the duration and size, then test it with the intended recipient or upload form.

Format, filename and size are separate checks

A form asks for MP3: select the converted .mp3 copy, not the original M4A. If it still fails, read the exact rejection: accepted format, maximum size and maximum duration are separate requirements. Conversion does not guarantee acceptance by every form.

The MP3 is larger than the M4A: conversion is not a size guarantee. Size depends on duration and encoding settings. If the destination accepts M4A and your original already works, you may not need conversion. If size is the problem, see how audio bitrate affects file size and making a smaller audio copy.

The source does not play: first check that the full file is downloaded and opens in an audio player. A converter is not a general repair tool for damaged or protected files. Export another playable copy from the original recording App when possible.

The saved copy sounds worse: return to the original and try a higher quality setting. Repeated lossy conversion cannot restore detail already lost. Avoid repeatedly converting the same MP3.

Other ways to handle the recording

If you only need a shorter recording, trim its edges instead; the App's trim workflow saves M4A. Apple's Voice Memos export instructions explain how to send or export a recording, but sharing alone is not this MP3 conversion workflow.

If you prefer a browser, try our free M4A-to-MP3 browser tool. Selected-file processing stays on your device. It is a separate tool, with different controls and device limits; keep the source and verify its output too.
