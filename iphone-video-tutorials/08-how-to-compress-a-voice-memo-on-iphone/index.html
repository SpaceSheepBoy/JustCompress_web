How to Compress a Voice Memo on iPhone

Make a large iPhone Voice Memo smaller so it is easier to send, upload, or keep.

Watch on YouTube

Quick steps

This is for iPhone users who want to finish the job without learning a big app. The video shows the full flow, and these steps keep it simple.

  1. Open Just Audio Compressor on your iPhone.
  2. Choose the Voice Memo or recording that is too large.
  3. Pick a smaller audio setting.
  4. Export the smaller file and send or upload it.

What app do I need?

Use Just Audio Compressor. You can open the App Store link, or search Just Audio Compressor in the App Store.
