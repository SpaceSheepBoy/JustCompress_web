How to trim audio on iPhone

Keep one useful section of a recording: choose its start and end, listen to both boundaries, then save a separate M4A clip.

Quick answer: In Audio Compressor MP3 Converter, open Trim, choose your audio file, set Start and End, preview the cut, then trim and tap Save audio. Keep your original until you have checked the saved copy.

Follow the steps · Watch the walkthrough · Other ways to trim

Trim the beginning and end of a recording

  1. Choose the file. Open Trim in Audio Compressor MP3 Converter and select your recording from Files. If it is in Voice Memos, share the recording and save it to Files first.
  2. Mark the part to keep. Drag the timeline boundaries or edit Start and End. Everything between them is kept; audio before Start and after End is left out of the exported copy.
  3. Listen before exporting. Use Play Start to check that the first word is complete and Play End to check that the last word is not cut off. Adjust the boundaries and listen again.
  4. Check the duration and trim. The output card shows Trimmed M4A and the duration kept. Start trimming and wait for the result. Trimming saves one continuous section; it does not remove a pause from the middle and join the surrounding speech.
  5. Save and verify the copy. Tap Save audio, choose a folder in Files, then open that saved file. Listen to its beginning and end and confirm its duration. Use Send audio if you want to share the result directly.

Example, not a measured export: Start at 10 seconds and end at 45 seconds to keep a 35-second clip. End is a position in the original recording, not the length of the new file.

Need MP3 or a smaller file?

The App’s trim workflow exports M4A, even if the input was MP3. If your destination specifically requires MP3, convert the saved M4A to MP3 afterward. Renaming the extension does not convert the audio.

A shorter recording may take less space, but its final size also depends on the output encoding. Trimming is not a target-size guarantee. If the full recording must be kept, use audio compression and bitrate settings instead. Lossy conversion cannot restore detail already lost in the original.

Choose the simplest tool for your recording

A recording already in Voice Memos: Apple’s built-in editor can trim its ends without installing another App. See Apple’s recording-editing instructions. Review the save options carefully and preserve a duplicate if you need the untouched recording.

A file you want to trim in a browser: use our free browser audio trimmer. It processes the file on your device without uploading it. This is a separate browser tool; its output and controls differ from the iPhone App.

Several separate clips: one trim keeps one continuous range. To break a recording into multiple files, use audio splitting. To remove speech or silence from the middle and rejoin the rest, choose an editor with that specific cutting workflow.

If the result is not what you expected

  • The wrong part remains: move Start and End around the section you want to keep. Do not select the part you want to discard.
  • The first or last word is clipped: widen the range slightly, preview both boundaries and export again from the original.
  • The receiving App rejects M4A: check its accepted formats and convert a copy if needed. A successful trim does not guarantee every destination accepts that format.
  • You cannot find the result: use Save audio and note the folder chosen in Files. Check the saved file before deleting or moving the original.
