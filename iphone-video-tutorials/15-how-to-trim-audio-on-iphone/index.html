How to trim audio on iPhone

Keep one useful section of a recording: choose its start and end, listen to both boundaries, then save a separate M4A clip.

Quick answer: In Audio Compressor MP3 Converter, open Trim, choose your audio file, set Start and End, preview the cut, then trim and tap Save audio. Keep your original until you have checked the saved copy.

Trim the beginning and end of a recording

  1. Choose the file. Open Trim and choose your recording from Files. For a Voice Memos recording, share a copy to Files first.
  2. Mark the part to keep. Drag the boundaries or edit Start and End. The range between them is kept; audio outside it is left out of the copy.
  3. Listen before exporting. Use Play Start and Play End. If a word is cut off, widen the range and listen again.
  4. Check the duration and trim. Check the Trimmed M4A duration, then start trimming. This keeps one continuous section; it does not cut a pause from the middle and rejoin speech.
  5. Save and verify the copy. Tap Save audio and choose a folder in Files. Open the saved copy, check its duration and listen to both ends before sharing.

Need MP3 or a smaller file?

The App’s trim workflow exports M4A, even if the input was MP3. If your destination specifically requires MP3, convert the saved M4A to MP3 afterward. Renaming the extension does not convert the audio.

Trimming does not guarantee a target file size. To keep the full recording while making it smaller, use audio compression and bitrate settings instead. Lossy conversion cannot restore lost detail.

Choose the simplest tool for your recording

A recording already in Voice Memos: Apple’s built-in editor can trim its ends. See Apple’s recording-editing instructions. Preserve a duplicate if you need the original.

A file you want to trim in a browser: use our free browser audio trimmer. It runs on your device without uploading the file. Its controls and output differ from the iPhone App.

Several separate clips: one trim keeps one continuous range. To break a recording into multiple files, use audio splitting. Removing a middle section and rejoining speech needs an editor with that cutting workflow.

If the result is not what you expected

  • The wrong part remains: move Start and End around the section you want to keep. Do not select the part you want to discard.
  • The first or last word is clipped: widen the range slightly, preview both boundaries and export again from the original.
  • The receiving App rejects M4A: check its accepted formats and convert a copy if needed. A successful trim does not guarantee every destination accepts that format.
  • You cannot find the result: use Save audio and note the folder chosen in Files. Check the saved file before deleting or moving the original.
