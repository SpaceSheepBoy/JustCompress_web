❓ Quick answers
How do I convert MOV to MP4?
  1. Choose a MOV, M4V, or short video file.
  2. Pick an MP4 quality setting.
  3. Click Convert to MP4. The MP4 downloads when ready.
Are videos uploaded?

No. The conversion runs locally in your browser.

Why not only rename .mov to .mp4?

Renaming only changes the filename. This tool exports an MP4 video file with H.264 video and AAC audio for broader compatibility.

Does your video look gray after sharing?

Check its HDR / SDR metadata before converting.
