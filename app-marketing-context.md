## Selection and goal

Net units include reported renewals and refunds; they are not unique purchasers or attributed website sales. Raw figures remain in private local evidence outside this public repository.

All three public US listings are free to download, with optional one-time Lifetime and auto-renewing Monthly purchases. VideoFix includes three successful conversions. Audio includes one successful export; recording/playback remain free. Combine Photos includes one free export and every layout. Current localized prices are shown by Apple before purchase.

Pages show iPhone interfaces and the verified iOS 17.0 minimum. This pass does not verify additional device layouts, regional availability or Mac release status. Existing app-specific policies remain separate and unchanged.

## Creative and source constraints

Reuse the approved two-column page rhythm, three actual app screens and official App Store badge. Keep each App’s palette, type and value proposition. Privacy belongs in relevant FAQ/legal details unless it is an independently established product selling point.

Screens are actual source captures used in published store artwork. WebP copies preserve decoded RGB pixels and source dimensions. Example media/settings are identified; VideoFix’s Log/SDR example uses matching supplied conversion frames and is distinguished from HDR. No synthetic ratings, download counts or conversion lift.
