What you can do

  • Map HDR highlights into an SDR photo for ordinary sharing.
  • Convert HEIC or device-compatible RAW photos to JPEG, or export PNG.
  • Apply a creative normal-to-HDR brightness effect.
  • Process batches and choose whether to retain capture date and location.
  • Keep Live Photo motion for normal SDR export; HDR export creates a still photo.

Access and purchases

The first 3 successful photo conversions are free. Every photo in a batch uses one conversion. Lifetime and monthly purchases unlock unlimited conversions.

Prices and availability can differ by storefront. Apple shows the current local offer before purchase.

Before you export or share

Normal-to-HDR cannot recover detail already lost. HDR brightness depends on the display. JPEG replaces transparency with white; PNG can preserve it.
