Local processing

PhotoFix processes your photos entirely on your iPhone. We do not collect your photos, personal information, usage analytics or advertising identifiers. No account is required. There are no advertising or analytics SDKs.

Photo access and files

You choose which photos or files to import. The system file picker provides access only to files you select. The in-app photo browser requests photo library access to show authorized HDR, RAW and HEIC photos; you can allow selected photos or all photos, or refuse access. Saving an export requests permission to add photos. Temporary import copies and completed exports are stored locally. Completed exports stay in the app’s Library until you delete them. Deleting the app removes its local data; copies saved to Photos or shared elsewhere remain.

Dates and location

When “Keep capture date & location” is enabled, existing capture dates and GPS metadata are copied into the exported file. Turn this setting off before conversion to omit those fields from the output. We do not access the device’s current location.

Purchases

Apple processes purchases and payments. PhotoFix checks verified App Store entitlements to unlock paid access. Apple’s privacy policy applies to payment processing. PhotoFix does not receive your payment card details.

Sharing and backups

Files you choose to share are handled by the destination app or service. Your device and iCloud backup settings may include local app data; these services are controlled by Apple and your device settings.

Changes

This page will be updated if the app’s privacy practices change. Questions about using the app are covered on the support page.
