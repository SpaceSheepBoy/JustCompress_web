中文版

核心承诺：「一座」不接入广告、第三方分析或跨应用追踪。修行数据默认保存在本机；如果您开启 iCloud 同步，数据会通过 Apple CloudKit 保存在您自己的 iCloud 账户中。只有当您主动创建或加入一个共修房间时，本应用才会向服务器发送数据；不参加共修的用户，本应用不进行任何网络传输。

概述

「一座」是一款专注于持咒计数、日课念诵与禅修定时的修行辅助工具。我们尊重并保护您的隐私。本隐私政策说明应用如何存储、同步和保护您的信息。

我们收集什么

我们不运营用户账户系统，不接入广告 SDK，不使用第三方分析服务。除「共修」功能外，不会把您的修行数据上传到 Space Sheep Studio 的服务器（见下方「共修」一节）。

应用会在设备本地保存您主动创建或产生的内容，例如咒语、发愿、计数记录、禅修记录、日课设置、主题与声音偏好。

数据存储与 iCloud 同步

  • 默认情况下，数据保存在您的 iPhone 或 iPad 本地。
  • 如果您开启 iCloud 同步，修行数据会通过 Apple CloudKit 存储在您的个人 iCloud 账户中，用于在自己的 Apple 设备之间同步。
  • iCloud 中的数据 Space Sheep Studio 无法读取；我们也不会将任何数据出售或共享给广告网络。
  • 关闭 iCloud 同步后，应用会停止主动向 iCloud 同步新的更改。

共修

「共修」让几位修行者把各自的念诵遍数汇到一处。这是本应用唯一会连接网络的功能，且完全可选。

  • 什么时候联网：只有当您主动创建或加入一个共修房间后，本应用才会向服务器发送数据。
  • 发送什么：房间标识、房间标题及选择的经咒标识；一个为该房间单独随机生成的匿名标识；一个设备标识；您在该房间的每日遍数及对应日期；系统分配给您的化名编号。
  • 不发送什么：您的姓名、邮箱、Apple ID、设备型号，以及您的其他咒语、禅修记录、发愿内容和自定义念诵文。
  • 化名：由系统从内置词库随机分配，您可以重新抽取。应用不接受用户自行输入昵称。
  • 谁能看到：只有持有该房间 6 位邀请码并已加入的人。房间不可搜索，没有公开列表。房主可以选择是否向房内其他人显示每个人的遍数；无论如何设置，您始终能看到自己的遍数。
  • 存在哪里、存多久：数据存储在 Cloudflare D1（Cloudflare, Inc. 作为数据处理者，其隐私政策见 cloudflare.com/privacypolicy）。房间在最后一次有人上报后 180 天进入回收，再过 30 天彻底删除。
  • 退出：在房间设置中退出即可，退出后您的化名不再显示在名录上。已经回向的遍数会保留在该房间的共修总数中。
  • 删除请求：由于共修完全匿名，我们无法凭姓名或邮箱找到您的数据。如需删除，请在房间设置中点「复制我的共修标识」，把复制出的内容发送到下方邮箱，我们会据此定位并删除。

English Version

Our promise: Mantra Counter & Meditation does not include ads, third-party analytics, or cross-app tracking. Practice data is stored locally by default. If you enable iCloud Sync, your data is stored in your own iCloud account through Apple CloudKit. The app sends data to a server only after you create or join a co-practice room. If you never use co-practice, the app makes no network transmissions at all.

Overview

Mantra Counter & Meditation is a practice companion for mantra counting, daily recitation, and meditation timing. We respect and protect your privacy. This policy explains how the app stores, syncs, and protects your information.

What We Collect

We do not operate user accounts, advertising SDKs, or third-party analytics. Apart from the co-practice feature, your practice data is never sent to Space Sheep Studio servers (see “Co-practice” below).

The app stores the content you create or generate on your device, such as mantras, vows, count records, meditation sessions, daily-practice settings, theme preferences, and sound preferences.

Data Storage and iCloud Sync

  • By default, data is stored locally on your iPhone or iPad.
  • If you enable iCloud Sync, practice data is stored in your personal iCloud account through Apple CloudKit so it can sync across your own Apple devices.
  • Space Sheep Studio cannot read data stored in your iCloud account, and we do not sell or share any data with advertising networks.
  • After iCloud Sync is turned off, the app stops actively syncing new changes to iCloud.

Co-practice

Co-practice lets a few practitioners pool their recitation counts in one place. It is the only feature in the app that uses the network, and it is entirely optional.

  • When the app connects: only after you create or join a co-practice room.
  • What is sent: the room identifier, room title, and selected mantra identifier; an anonymous identifier generated separately for each room; a device identifier; your daily recitation counts in that room with their dates; and the number of the pseudonym the system assigned to you.
  • What is not sent: your name, email, Apple ID, or device model, or your other mantras, meditation records, vows, or custom prayers.
  • Pseudonyms: assigned at random by the system from a built-in word list; you may draw another. The app does not accept user-entered nicknames.
  • Who can see it: only people who hold the room’s 6-character invite code and have joined. Rooms cannot be searched and are not listed anywhere. The host may choose whether each person’s count is shown to others; you can always see your own.
  • Where it is stored and for how long: in Cloudflare D1 (Cloudflare, Inc. acts as data processor; see cloudflare.com/privacypolicy). A room is retired 180 days after the last count is added, and fully deleted 30 days after that.
  • Leaving: leave from the room settings; your pseudonym no longer appears in the list. Recitations you have already dedicated remain in the room’s shared total.
  • Deletion requests: because co-practice is fully anonymous, we cannot find your data by name or email. To request deletion, tap “Copy my co-practice ID” in the room settings and email what is copied to the address below.

Widgets and Live Activities

The Home Screen widget and Live Activities use Apple platform features to read or display your current count, timer, and session state. This is used to continue practice from the Lock Screen or Home Screen, not for tracking.

Permissions

  • Photos: Used only when you save a share card to Photos or choose a file/image for import.
  • Notifications: Used only for local holy-day or smoke-offering reminders if you enable them.
  • Audio: Used to play counting sounds, meditation bells, and recitation audio.
  • iCloud: Used only when you enable iCloud Sync for cross-device practice-data sync.

Third-Party Services

The app does not use third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking services. Co-practice data is stored on our behalf by Cloudflare (see “Co-practice”). External source links may open websites governed by their own privacy policies.

Export, Backup, and Sharing

The app provides data export and share-card features. Exported files and share images are saved only to a location you choose or passed to apps you select through the system share sheet.
