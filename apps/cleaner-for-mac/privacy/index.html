Cleaner for Mac: Disk Map Privacy Policy

Space Sheep Studio · Effective November 21, 2026

Files and settings

The app reads folders you select on your Mac. File names, sizes, duplicate fingerprints, model file details and scan results are processed on your device. Space Sheep Studio does not receive them. Selected files move to the system Trash when you confirm. You control recovery and removal in Finder.

Your language and sound preferences are saved locally. The app has no developer account sign-in, advertising SDK, tracking or analytics.

Purchases

Apple processes purchases through the App Store. The app uses StoreKit on your Mac to check product prices and verified entitlements. Space Sheep Studio does not receive your payment card details. Apple's own services are subject to Apple’s privacy policy.

Support and contact

If you email support@justcompress.online, we receive your email address and anything you choose to send. We use it to answer your request and keep it only as needed to resolve and follow up on that request, or as legally required. You may request deletion through the same address.

隐私政策

本应用仅在你的 Mac 上处理你所选文件夹中的名称、大小、重复文件指纹、模型文件信息和扫描结果。Space Sheep Studio 不会收到这些信息。经你确认的文件会移至系统废纸篓，可在访达中恢复或清空。语言和声音偏好保存在本机。应用无需注册，没有广告、跟踪或分析 SDK。

Apple 通过 App Store 处理付款。应用在本机通过 StoreKit 检查商品价格和已验证的购买权益。我们不会收到支付卡信息。主动联系技术支持时，我们会收到你的邮箱和你提供的内容，用于回复和跟进，并根据处理请求和法律要求保留。你可以发邮件要求删除这类资料。
