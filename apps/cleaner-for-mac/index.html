Cleaner for Mac Support

Choose a folder to review large files, duplicate copies and cleanup candidates. Open a result to inspect its location and select the items you want to move to Trash.

Recover an item

Open Trash in Finder. Select the file and choose File → Put Back when available. Files remain in Trash until you or macOS empty it. Moving files to Trash may leave available disk space unchanged.

Purchases

Disk Map and Model Files require Pro. Open the Pro panel and choose Restore Purchases while signed in to the Apple Account used for your purchase. You can manage a subscription in your App Store account settings.

Contact

Email support@justcompress.online with your app version, macOS version and the steps you took. Remove personal file names from any screenshot you choose to send.

技术支持

选择一个文件夹后，可以检查大文件、重复副本和待清理项目。先查看文件位置，再确认移到废纸篓。要恢复文件，请在访达中打开废纸篓，选择文件，再点可用的“放回原处”。腾出实际磁盘空间取决于何时清空废纸篓。

磁盘分布和模型文件工具需要专业版。使用购买时的 Apple 账户登录，在专业版面板点“恢复购买”。如需帮助，请通过上方邮箱联系我们。
