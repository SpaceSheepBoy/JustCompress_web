CamRecorder: Camera Recorder

Record your screen and camera together on Mac

US App Store version 1.0 · macOS 14.0 or later · Checked 2026-11-06

What you can do

  • Choose Camera, Window + Camera, or Full Screen + Camera.
  • Place the camera in any corner and adjust its size.
  • Mix computer audio and your microphone with separate volume controls and optional echo cancellation.
  • Choose export size and frame rate, including 720p, 1080p, 4K and custom dimensions.
  • Take JPG photos, save to your chosen folder, and start or stop recording from the menu bar.

Access and purchases

Free use includes a cumulative total of 60 minutes of video recording without Plus effects, plus ordinary photos. The allowance does not reset daily. CamRecorder Plus is a one-time purchase for unlimited recording, local background blur and Screen Light. Effects can be previewed for free. See the App Store for the current local price.

Prices and availability can differ by storefront. Apple shows the current local offer before purchase.

Before you export or share

Requires macOS 14 or later. Source quality and frame rate depend on your devices; resizing an export does not add source detail. Blur and echo cancellation depend on hardware and the recording environment. Screen Light color temperatures are approximate.
