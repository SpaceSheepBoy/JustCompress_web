How do I record?

Choose Camera, Window + Camera, or Full Screen + Camera. For screen recording, select the window or display in the macOS picker. Place the camera in a corner and adjust its size. Choose your microphone, computer-audio volume, export size and frame rate, then select Record video. Select Stop & save to finish. Videos save automatically as MOV files to your chosen folder. Export options include 720p, 1080p, 4K and custom dimensions; source quality depends on your devices. Optional echo cancellation can help when recording computer audio with your microphone.

Can I take photos?

Use the camera button or Command-P. Photos save as JPG files in the same folder. You can also take a photo while recording.

Where are my files?

Click the saved confirmation to reveal your latest capture in Finder. The menu bar also offers Open recordings folder.

Screen, camera or microphone unavailable?

Open System Settings → Privacy & Security. Allow CamRecorder under Screen & System Audio Recording for screen and computer audio, Camera for camera capture, and Microphone for microphone audio. Labels can differ by macOS version. Reopen the app if macOS asks, then select your source again.

What is free?

Free use includes a cumulative total of 60 minutes of video recording without Plus effects, plus ordinary photos. The allowance does not reset daily. Plus provides unlimited video recording, local background blur and Screen Light. Both effects can be previewed for free. Plus is a one-time purchase, with no subscription; Apple shows the current local price before purchase. Apple system video effects do not require Plus, but recordings still count toward the free allowance.

How do I restore Plus?

Use the Apple Account that purchased Plus and select Restore purchase in the Plus window. If the product cannot load, check your connection and select Retry.

How does screen light work?

CamRecorder uses a display as a light source. It prefers an external display when connected; otherwise it uses the built-in display. Adjust the color, brightness, and light-window size in the app. Color temperatures are approximate presets.

Why does blur vary?

Background blur uses local person segmentation. Lighting, quick movement, hair, and objects crossing the person can affect edges. Adjust the strength before recording. Blur settings are frozen during a recording.

Can I record from the menu bar?

After setup, start recording or select Stop & save from the menu bar camera icon. Closing an idle main window keeps the app available in the menu bar. Select Quit CamRecorder to exit. Pause is not supported.

Requirements

macOS 14 or later. Capture resolution depends on your camera.

Need more help?
