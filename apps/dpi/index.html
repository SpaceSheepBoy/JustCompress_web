DPI

Set photos to 300 DPI in one tap. Check the print size, fit photos to a paper size, change up to 50 photos at once, then print or email the result. JPEG, PNG and HEIC, all on your iPhone with no upload and no account.

The DPI tag changes without re-encoding your photo. After export, DPI, pixel size and file size are read back from the new file.
