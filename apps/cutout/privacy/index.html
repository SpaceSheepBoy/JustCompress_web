Easy Background Remover Privacy Policy

Effective November 25, 2026

Easy Background Remover appears as Cutout on your device. Space Sheep Studio does not collect personal data through the app.

Photos and camera

You can select photos through the iOS photo picker or grant camera access to take a picture. The app uses Apple Vision on your device to remove backgrounds. Space Sheep Studio does not receive your photos, cutouts or edits.

Local files and exports

The app stores temporary working copies on your device. When you start a new batch, it deletes the old batch’s working copies. You can remove remaining app files by deleting the app.

Saving an export requires permission to add images to Photos. Delete any saved images in Photos. Your iCloud Photos settings control whether Apple syncs your photo library.

Free exports and purchases

The app keeps a count of the free exports used in local settings and the device’s Keychain. The Keychain can keep the count on this device after reinstalling the app.

Apple handles subscriptions, one-time purchases and restores under the Apple Privacy Policy. The app checks verified App Store transactions to unlock paid access. Space Sheep Studio does not receive your payment card details.

Accounts and tracking

The app does not require a Space Sheep Studio account. It includes no ads or analytics trackers.

Your choices

You can change the app’s camera and Photos permissions in iOS Settings. For privacy questions, contact support@justcompress.online.
