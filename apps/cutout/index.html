Easy Background Remover

Remove photo backgrounds and save white-background product photos or transparent PNGs

US App Store version 1.0 · iOS 17.0 or later · Checked 2026-11-26

What you can do

  • Remove photo backgrounds on your iPhone. Your photos stay on your device during processing.
  • Edit one photo or process up to 50 at once. The default batch export centers each subject on a 1200 × 1200 white canvas.
  • Choose a background color or use a transparent background. Save transparent PNGs or solid-background JPGs to Photos.
  • Choose a preset canvas size for a product listing or social post, or set your own dimensions.
  • Drag or pinch to position a subject. Refine its edges with the erase and restore brushes.

Access and purchases

The first three exports are free. A successful batch save counts as one export. A monthly subscription or a one-time purchase unlocks unlimited exports.

Prices and availability can differ by storefront. Apple shows the current local offer before purchase.

Before you export or share

Choose PNG when you need transparency. Choose JPG for a solid background. Preview the cutout edges and canvas size before saving.
