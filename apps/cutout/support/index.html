Easy Background Remover Support

Easy Background Remover appears as Cutout on your iPhone.

Edit and save a photo

  1. Tap Edit one photo to choose an image or take a photo. Tap Batch photos to process several photos together.
  2. Choose a background color and canvas size. In the single-photo editor, move or resize the subject, or open Refine Cutout to adjust the edges.
  3. Tap Save to add the result to Photos. Choose a transparent background for PNG or a solid background for JPG.

When a photo won't open or save

  • Open an iCloud photo in Photos to download it first, then choose it again in Cutout.
  • If Cutout misses part of the subject, use the Restore brush in Refine Cutout.
  • If saving fails, allow Cutout to add photos in iPhone Settings and check available storage.

Purchases

Your first three successful exports are free. Saving a batch counts as one export. A monthly subscription or one-time purchase unlocks unlimited exports.

Open the plans screen from Home and tap Restore purchases using the Apple Account you bought with. Manage a subscription or get billing help through Apple Support.
