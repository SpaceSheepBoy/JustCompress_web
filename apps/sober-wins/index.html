Before you begin

A few useful answers.

Is Sober Wins free?

Basic tracking, history, collectibles, the mini fridge and basic poster sharing are free. Pro adds premium nine-position cabinets, poster export and a 4× daily collection boost. Apple shows the current local offer before purchase.

Do I need an account?

No app account is required. Tracking records and cabinet arrangements stay on your iPhone. There is no developer cloud sync. Apple device backups depend on your settings; restoring a purchase does not restore deleted history.

What if I have a drink or make a mistake?

Record what happened and continue using your history. Use the correction controls in History for an incorrect daily entry. Your record includes more than your current alcohol-free period.

Do I have to connect Apple HealthKit?

No. Sleep and resting-heart-rate trends are optional and require your permission. You can use tracking and collection without them. Health trends are not medical advice or proof that a change was caused by alcohol.

What are the collectibles?

They are virtual, illustrated beer items that you can collect and arrange. They have no cash value and cannot be redeemed for alcohol. This visual theme may not suit everyone; check the screenshots before choosing the app.

Does the Panic button call for help?

No. It opens in-app pause activities, including breathing. It does not contact emergency services. The optional camera mirror is not required for breathing. Sober Wins does not provide treatment for alcohol withdrawal.

Is Lifetime a subscription?

Lifetime is a one-time Pro purchase. Existing active Monthly subscriptions also provide Pro while valid. Lifetime does not cancel a Monthly subscription; manage any subscription separately in your Apple Account.

Can I use the tracker without collecting items?

Yes. Use the daily No alcohol or Had a drink choices and review History. The collection adds a visual part to the routine, but arranging your mini fridge does not need to be your focus. Choose the parts of the app that are useful to you.

Is a missing entry the same as an alcohol-free day?

No. A day you have not recorded and a day you have confirmed as alcohol-free are different. Review the actual entries in History before interpreting a counter or a pattern. Use the available correction controls for an incorrect entry.

Does a drinking day erase the rest of my record?

Your history gives context beyond the current alcohol-free period. Record what actually happened and review your earlier entries rather than treating one counter as your entire record. Collection rewards are not a reason to change the answer you log.

Do the collectibles represent real drinks or money?

No. They are virtual illustrated items with no cash value and no alcohol redemption. The beer-item theme is visible in the screenshots and may not suit everyone choosing an alcohol-free tracker. Inspect it before deciding whether this is your kind of app.

How is a mini fridge different from a day counter?

The day counter summarizes a period, while the mini fridge is a place to arrange your virtual collection. They serve different roles in the app. The collection can make the routine more visual, but it is not a clinical measure or proof of recovery.

Can I share a milestone without sharing my whole history?

The app includes basic poster sharing, while Pro adds poster export. Inspect the poster and its visible details before choosing a destination. Sharing a selected image is different from publishing your entire history; choose what you want others to see.

What does Restore Purchases bring back?

It restores eligible Pro access associated with your Apple Account. It does not recover deleted daily entries, collection items or cabinet arrangements. Device backups depend on your Apple settings, so keep important exports before removing the app.

Does the browser breathing timer update my app?

No. The timer on this page is a separate one-minute web exercise. It does not connect to Sober Wins, record a daily entry, grant collectibles or change your purchases. Stop whenever you want and follow a comfortable pace.

Is this a treatment or a support community?

Sober Wins is a daily tracking and reflection app with virtual collectibles and pause activities. It does not provide medical treatment, emergency response or a substitute for professional support. Choose the wider support that fits your situation.
