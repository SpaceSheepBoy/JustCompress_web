How to share a little win from Sober Wins

Choose a moment, review the poster and decide what you want to share.

By Space Sheep Studio · October 26, 2026 · iPhone app version 1.2

In this guide

  • Sharing is your choice
  • Start with the record and collection you have
  • Review the finished poster before sending
  • Know what is included
  • A poster is a moment, not a full record

Sharing is your choice

A daily record can be useful without being public. If you want to mark a moment, Sober Wins includes basic poster sharing. You can also keep the image for yourself. There is no need to share a day, a collection or a number to make the record count.

Start with the record and collection you have

Review your daily log and the virtual items you have collected. Choose what you want to show using the app’s poster controls. The illustrated beer collectibles are virtual items, have no cash value and cannot be redeemed for alcohol. Consider whether that visual theme is appropriate for the audience you want to share with.

Review the finished poster before sending

Inspect the text, images and any information that identifies your routine. Confirm that the poster says what you intend it to say. Use the preview to check the image before choosing a destination, then inspect the saved or sent copy afterwards.

Know what is included

Basic tracking, history, collectibles, the mini fridge and basic poster sharing are free. Pro adds premium nine-position cabinets, poster export and a four-times daily collection boost. Check the current local offer in the app before purchasing. The exact sharing controls can vary with the selected language.

A poster is a moment, not a full record

A shared image does not contain the context of every daily entry, and it is not proof of recovery or a medical assessment. Keep the daily record useful for you, whether or not you decide to show a particular moment to someone else.
