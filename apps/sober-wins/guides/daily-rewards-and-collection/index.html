How daily rewards work in Sober Wins

Turn daily check-ins into a virtual collection, then arrange your finds in the mini fridge.

By Space Sheep Studio · Published October 26, 2026 · Sober Wins 1.2

A small reason to come back

Sober Wins pairs a daily alcohol log with virtual collectibles. The collection adds something visual to a routine that otherwise consists of dates and numbers. It is optional: you can use the tracker without arranging a cabinet.

The collectibles are illustrated beer items. Consider whether that theme suits you before choosing the app; it will not suit everyone looking for an alcohol-free tracker.

From a daily entry to your collection

  1. Log your day. On the home screen, choose No alcohol or Had a drink. Record the true answer; collection points are not a reason to change your log.
  2. Check reward progress. The home screen shows progress toward the next collectible.
  3. Open Collection. Review your unlocked items and new finds when available.
  4. Arrange your mini fridge. Use the cabinet controls to place your collected items and revisit your arrangement.

What is free, and what does Pro add?

Basic tracking, history, collectibles, the mini fridge and basic poster sharing are free. Pro adds premium nine-position cabinets, poster export and a 4× daily collection boost.

Lifetime is a one-time purchase. Existing active Monthly subscriptions also provide Pro while valid. Buying Lifetime does not cancel an existing subscription; manage that separately in your Apple Account. Check the current local offer in the app before buying.

What the rewards mean

These are virtual collection items. They have no cash value and cannot be redeemed for alcohol. A cabinet is not a medical measure or proof of recovery.

Your collection and arrangement are stored on your device. Restoring a purchase restores eligible paid access, not deleted collectibles or local records. For purchase help, use the Sober Wins support page.
