What the service does

TalkNice transcribes recordings, composes messages in the selected output language, and provides optional kinder rewrites. AI results may contain mistakes. Review the result before copying, inserting or sending it. TalkNice does not send messages automatically and is not professional counselling, therapy or advice. Cloud features require network access. Keyboard dictation requires enabling a microphone session in the main app, then returning to a compatible text field to dictate using the TalkNice keyboard.

Free plan and Pro

The free plan includes 10 completed dictations or keyboard rewrites per day. A completed dictation includes its selected output language and an optional kinder rewrite. Failed requests do not use the daily app allowance. Pro removes the daily app allowance; recordings remain limited to 2 minutes each, and infrastructure safety limits and service availability still apply.

Pro offers one-month and one-year auto-renewing subscriptions. The exact price, currency, billing period and any eligible offer are displayed in the app and Apple's purchase confirmation before purchase. The displayed store price governs your purchase.

  • If an eligible free trial is offered, it converts to the displayed paid subscription unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the trial ends. Eligibility is determined by Apple; unused trial time may be forfeited when purchasing a subscription.
  • Manage or cancel in Apple Account subscription settings. Cancellation prevents the next renewal; paid access ordinarily continues until the current period ends.
  • Restore purchases in the app using the Apple Account that purchased them. Refund requests are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
