TalkNice Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

TalkNice turns recorded speech into text, offers optional kinder rewrites, and can compose mixed-language speech in your selected output language. Space Sheep Studio operates the service. Contact: support@kilalabs.com.

Cloud AI permission and usage identifier

Before cloud processing, TalkNice asks you to allow sharing recorded audio with Groq and requested text with OpenAI or DeepSeek, including regional or fallback processing. This choice covers the app, history rewrites and keyboard. Declining prevents cloud requests; basic typing and local history remain available. Review or withdraw this permission in Settings. A random installation identifier accompanies requests for usage limits and abuse prevention; it is not an advertising identifier. Operational usage counters and technical request information may be retained. No advertising or cross-app tracking is used.

Audio and text processing

With your cloud AI permission, audio may be uploaded in segments while you record and sent through our transcription service to Groq for speech recognition. Short recordings and recovery requests may upload the full recording after you stop. Cancel stops further uploads but cannot recall audio already received by a provider. If you choose a different output language, your transcript is sent to an AI provider for translation. Tapping Make it kinder sends the transcript for a rewrite. A rewrite you request from the keyboard also sends up to about 400 characters of the text immediately before and after what is being rewritten, so the result can match its intent; your wider typing history is not read. These cloud features require an internet connection and process the content only to fulfill your requested operation.

In password, verification-code and card-number fields TalkNice sends no surrounding text and offers no rewrite. A rewrite you start from the History screen sends only the saved text you chose. Rewrites also carry the expression style and strength you selected.

The keyboard's preliminary tone detection runs on your device. Optional Apple Speech Recognition uses on-device recognition when available and authorized. Recording takes place in the main app. TalkNice does not send messages to other people automatically.

Service providers and international processing

We use Groq for transcription and OpenAI or DeepSeek for text processing, depending on region, configuration and service availability. Cloudflare and Alibaba Cloud provide hosting and network infrastructure. Requests may be processed outside your country. Providers handle data under their applicable service terms, privacy policies and account settings. We do not claim zero cloud retention: providers may retain content or operational data for security, abuse prevention or legal obligations.

Provider information: Groq data handling, OpenAI privacy, DeepSeek.

Local history and recordings

Transcripts and rewrites are saved on your device by default. In History you can delete entries, clear history, choose 7-day or 30-day retention, or turn history off. Turning it off deletes the history file. The history file is protected and excluded from device backup.

The app deletes temporary audio after a successful result. Failed or stopped processing keeps audio locally for retry until you discard it, replace it, or next launch the app. Stopping a request prevents a late result from changing your screen; it cannot undo processing already received by a provider.

Technical data and payments

Our infrastructure processes technical request data such as IP address, request time, model, byte counts, token usage, errors and estimated processing cost for delivery, security and service operations. App subscription entitlement and daily free usage are stored locally. Apple handles payments; we do not receive your payment card details.

Keyboard Full Access and clipboard

Full Access enables network rewrites and sharing dictation requests and results with the main app. Basic typing works without it. Text is copied to the system clipboard only when you tap Copy. Other apps and system clipboard features may then access copied text under their own policies.

Optional on-device learning

“Learn from my edits” is off by default. When you turn it on, TalkNice notices on this device whether you kept or changed what it inserted, and keeps a short list of the words you use. Those words are attached to your later transcription requests as a recognition hint, so names and jargon come back the way you write them, and are used for nothing else. The list stays on your device, is never shared with other apps and is not used for advertising. Turning the switch off stops future requests from carrying it, and “Clear learned terms” in Settings deletes it. Counts of whether rewrites were kept are also stored locally and never uploaded. Learning needs the same cloud AI permission as transcription, and a new consent revision is required before it sends anything.

Your choices

You can decline microphone or Speech Recognition permission, disable keyboard Full Access, keep output in the original languages, avoid optional rewrites, and delete local history. Without microphone permission you cannot record; without cloud processing you cannot obtain final transcription. You can remove the keyboard and uninstall the app. No TalkNice account is required.

We do not sell your data or use your content for advertising. For privacy questions or access, correction or deletion requests where applicable, contact us. If a request concerns a local history file, use the app's deletion controls. We cannot guarantee deletion of provider records subject to their legal or security retention obligations.

Children and policy updates

TalkNice is not directed to children under 13. We will update this page when our practices change.

TalkNice 隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 10 月 14 日

TalkNice 将录音转为文字，提供可选的温柔改写，并可把混合语言转为完整英语。服务由 Space Sheep Studio 运营。联系：support@kilalabs.com。

云端 AI 授权与用量标识

使用云端处理前，TalkNice 会请求明确授权：将主动录制的语音发送给 Groq，将请求翻译或改写的文字发送给 OpenAI 或 DeepSeek，包括地区路由与备用处理。此选择覆盖主 App、历史记录改写和键盘。拒绝授权不会发出云端请求；基本输入和本地历史仍可使用。可在设置中查看或撤回授权。请求附带随机安装标识，用于用量限制和防滥用，并非广告标识。运行用量计数和技术请求信息可能被保留。我们不使用广告或跨 App 跟踪。

语音与文字处理

获得你的云端 AI 同意后，录音期间可能会分段上传音频，经我们的转写服务发送给 Groq 进行识别。短录音和恢复请求可能会在停止后上传完整录音。取消会停止后续上传，但无法撤回服务商已收到的音频。选择英语输出时，转写文字会发送给 AI 服务商翻译。点选 Make it kinder 时，转写文字会发送用于改写。你在键盘中主动发起的改写，还会一并发送被改写文字前后各最多约 400 个字符，使结果更贴合上下文；更早的输入记录不会被读取。这些云端功能需要联网，内容用于完成你请求的操作。

在密码、一次性验证码和卡号输入框中，TalkNice 不会发送任何上下文文字，也不会提供改写。从历史记录发起的改写只发送你选中的已保存文字。改写还会一并发送你选择的表达模式与强度。

键盘的初步语气检测在设备本地完成。允许 Apple 语音识别且设备支持时，可选的实时预览强制使用本地识别。录音在主 App 内进行。TalkNice 不会自动向他人发送消息。

第三方服务与跨境处理

转写使用 Groq；文字处理根据地区、配置与服务可用性使用 OpenAI 或 DeepSeek。Cloudflare 和阿里云提供托管与网络基础设施。请求可能在你所在国家或地区之外处理。服务商按其服务条款、隐私政策及账户设置处理数据。我们不承诺云端零保留：服务商可能因安全、防滥用或法律义务保留内容或运行数据。

服务商信息：Groq 数据处理、OpenAI 隐私政策、DeepSeek。

本地历史与录音

转写文字和改写默认保存在设备上。你可以删除单条、清空历史、选择保留 7 天或 30 天，或者关闭历史。关闭历史会删除历史文件。历史文件有文件保护，且不参与设备备份。

结果成功后，App 删除临时录音。处理失败或主动停止时，录音保留在本地供重试，直到放弃、替换录音或下次启动 App。停止请求可避免迟到的结果改变界面，但无法撤销服务商已经收到的处理。

技术数据与付款

基础设施会处理 IP 地址、请求时间、模型、字节数、token 用量、错误和估计处理费用等技术数据，用于请求传递、安全与服务运行。订阅权益和每日免费次数保存在本地。付款由 Apple 处理，我们不会收到银行卡信息。

键盘完全访问与剪贴板

完全访问用于联网改写及在键盘和主 App 之间共享听写请求与结果。不授予时仍可基础打字。只有点击复制，文字才写入系统剪贴板；其他 App 和系统剪贴板功能之后可能按各自政策访问该文字。

可选的端上学习

“从我的修改中学习”默认关闭。开启后，TalkNice 仅在设备本地判断你是否保留或修改了插入的内容，并保存一份你常用词语的简短清单。这些词语会附加在你之后的转写请求中作为识别提示，让人名和术语按你的写法被识别，除此之外不作其他用途。该清单只保存在设备上，不与其他 App 共享，也不用于广告。关闭开关后，之后的请求不再附带该清单；在设置中点击“清除已学词条”会立即删除它。是否保留改写的统计同样只保存在本地，绝不上传。学习功能与转写一样需要云端 AI 授权，并且在它开始发送内容前会先要求你重新同意。

你的选择与权利

你可以拒绝麦克风或语音识别权限、关闭完全访问、保留原语言输出、不使用可选改写，以及删除本地历史。没有麦克风权限时不能录音，不使用云端处理时不能获得最终转写。你可以移除键盘或卸载 App。TalkNice 不要求创建账户。

我们不出售数据，不将内容用于广告。如有隐私问题，或希望行使适用的访问、更正、删除权利，请联系我们。本地历史可直接通过 App 删除；我们无法保证删除服务商因法律或安全义务需要保留的记录。

儿童与政策更新
