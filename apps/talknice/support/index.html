TalkNice Support

Contact support@justcompress.online. Please include your app version, iOS version and steps to reproduce the issue. Do not send passwords, verification codes or sensitive recordings.

Dictation and keyboard

Record in TalkNice. For chat insertion, enable the TalkNice keyboard and Full Access, open TalkNice and enable a keyboard dictation session, then return to your chat to start and finish recording. Preview the transcript, optionally rewrite it, then tap Insert text. Edit directly in the destination app. End the session to release the microphone. Choose an output language on the main screen or in Settings to compose messages in that language. Mixed-language speech is supported. If processing fails, retry saved audio or use the original transcript when available.

Reply to a message

Enable automatic replies after reviewing its separate consent. Copy a received message and return to Native AF (TalkNice); allow iOS paste permission if requested. Review the suggestion, tap Add a voice instruction to refine it, and check the translation below Your reply. Understand message explains the original. Change Explain in through Reply options. The app never sends the reply to your correspondent. Saved context is optional and can be disabled in Reply options; manage saved replies and history in the app.

Subscriptions

Restore purchases in TalkNice Settings. Manage or cancel subscriptions through Apple. Request refunds through Apple.

TalkNice 支持

联系 support@justcompress.online，请说明 App 版本、iOS 版本及复现步骤。不要发送密码、验证码或敏感录音。

听写与键盘

在 TalkNice 内录音。聊天插入需开启 TalkNice 键盘与完全访问，在主 App 中启用键盘听写会话，再回到聊天键盘开始和结束录音。预览转写、按需改写，再点插入文本；在目标 App 中直接编辑，结束会话即可释放麦克风。在主界面或设置中选择输出语言，可将口述内容整理成该语言的消息，也支持混合语言口述。处理失败时可重试保存的录音；已有转写时可选择原文。

回复来信

阅读单独授权说明后启用自动回复。复制对方的消息，回到 Native AF（TalkNice）；若 iOS 询问，请允许粘贴。查看建议，用语音补充要求，并核对回复下方的译文；“理解消息”可解释原来的来信。可在回复选项中更改理解语言。App 不会自动给对方发送回复；可在回复选项中关闭历史上下文，并在 App 中管理已保存回复和历史。

订阅

在 TalkNice 设置中恢复购买。通过 Apple 管理或取消订阅；通过 Apple 申请退款。
