What you can do

  • Generate speech captions, review the transcript and edit the words.
  • Use emoji matching, animated caption styles and word highlights.
  • With Pro, caption up to 20 videos in a batch.
  • Use optional AI Correct and AI Translate, with consent, and export video or SRT text.

Access and purchases

Free single-video exports have a Just Captions watermark. One single-video Emoji export is available to try. Pro adds batch captions, locked styles, higher export quality and 600 AI caption minutes per month.

Prices and availability can differ by storefront. Apple shows the current local offer before purchase.

Before you export or share

Core caption generation can run on device. Optional online AI text tools send caption text after consent; they are different from the local caption workflow.
