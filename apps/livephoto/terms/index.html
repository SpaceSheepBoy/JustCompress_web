Free use and subscriptions

Three successful conversions are free in total, shared across both directions and all formats. Each successfully converted file uses one conversion; saving, sharing, cancellation and failed conversion do not consume another. The used count may persist across reinstallations on the same device. An active Pro subscription allows unlimited conversions.

Monthly and yearly plans offer the same Pro features. The purchase screen shows the localized total price and billing period before confirmation. Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Apple may charge renewal within 24 hours before the period ends. Manage or cancel in Apple Account subscription settings; cancellation preserves access for the remaining paid period. There is no subscription free trial. Refunds are handled by Apple.

Media and support

Only convert media you are entitled to use. Video-to-Live-Photo output uses a selected clip up to three seconds. Resolution depends on the source; 4K output requires a 4K source. Device and destination support may vary. Contact support@kilalabs.com. See the Privacy Policy.
