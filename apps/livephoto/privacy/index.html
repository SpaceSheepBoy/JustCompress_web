Live Photo · Privacy Policy

Updated October 15, 2026

Space Sheep Studio provides Live Photo to Video: 4K (also displayed as Live Photo). Contact: support@justcompress.online.

Photos, videos and processing

Conversion runs on your device using Apple frameworks. The app does not upload your photos, videos, audio or exports to Space Sheep Studio or third-party AI services. It does not perform face recognition, collect face data, or include advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. No app account is required.

Photos and Files permissions

You choose media with the system Photos or Files picker. Photo library access is used to load selected Live Photo resources; add permission is used to save results. You can refuse or limit access in iOS Settings. Apple may download originals from iCloud Photos, and a Files provider may download a selected file, under their own settings and policies. This is separate from local conversion.

Local storage and retention

Imported copies, temporary working files, export history and exported media are stored locally. Files you save or share are handled by your chosen destination. Exports in the app Documents folder can be managed in Files. Uninstalling removes the app container; copies saved to Photos, Files, backups or other apps must be managed separately.

The app offers three successful free conversions shared across both conversion directions and formats. The used count is stored in local preferences and the device Keychain for quota enforcement and may persist after reinstalling. It is not uploaded to us. The app has no control to reset this counter. Subscription state is checked through Apple StoreKit.

Payments and Apple services

Apple processes subscriptions, payments and restoration. The app verifies transactions on device; Space Sheep Studio does not receive payment card details or run a purchase server for this app. Apple handles data under its privacy policy. Device backups and iCloud features depend on your Apple settings.

Website and support

Opening these public pages shares ordinary request information, including IP address, with the website hosting infrastructure (GitHub Pages). If you email support, we receive the information you send and retain it as needed to resolve the request and meet legal obligations. Please avoid attaching private media unless necessary. We do not sell personal data.

Your choices and questions

You can change media permissions, delete local exports, manage your Apple subscription and contact support@justcompress.online about privacy, access or deletion requests. We cannot delete files stored only on your device or records controlled by Apple. This app is not specifically directed to children. We update this page when practices change.

Live Photo · 隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 10 月 15 日

Space Sheep Studio 提供 Live Photo to Video: 4K（设备上也显示为 Live Photo）。联系：support@justcompress.online。

照片、视频与处理

转换通过 Apple 框架在设备本地完成。App 不会将照片、视频、音频或导出结果上传至 Space Sheep Studio 或第三方 AI 服务，不做人脸识别或收集人脸数据，不含广告、分析或跟踪 SDK，无需创建 App 账户。

相册与文件权限

你通过系统相册或文件选择器选择媒体。读取相册权限用于加载所选实况照片资源，添加权限用于保存结果。你可在 iOS 设置中拒绝或限制权限。Apple 可能按你的设置从 iCloud 照片下载原件，文件提供商也可能下载所选文件；这些行为适用相应服务的政策，与本地转换分开。

本地存储与保留

导入副本、临时工作文件、导出历史和媒体保存在本地。保存或分享后的文件由你选择的目标处理。App Documents 中的导出文件可通过“文件”管理。卸载会删除 App 容器；保存到相册、文件、备份或其他 App 的副本需要单独管理。

两个转换方向及不同格式共用三次成功免费转换。已用次数保存在本地偏好设置及设备钥匙串中，用于额度管理，重装后可能保留，不会上传给我们。App 不提供重置此计数的操作。订阅状态通过 Apple StoreKit 验证。

付款与 Apple 服务

Apple 处理订阅、付款及恢复购买。App 在设备上验证交易；Space Sheep Studio 不接收银行卡资料，也没有此 App 的购买服务器。Apple 按其隐私政策处理数据。备份及 iCloud 功能取决于你的 Apple 设置。

网站与支持

打开公共页面时，网站托管基础设施（GitHub Pages）会处理 IP 地址等普通请求信息。如果你发送支持邮件，我们会接收主动提供的信息，并按解决问题及法律义务所需保留。请避免不必要地附上私人媒体。我们不出售个人数据。

你的选择

你可更改媒体权限、删除本地导出文件、管理 Apple 订阅，或联系 support@justcompress.online 提出隐私、访问及删除请求。我们无法删除只在你设备上的文件或由 Apple 控制的记录。本 App 并非专为儿童设计。处理方式变化时会更新本政策。
