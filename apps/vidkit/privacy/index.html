What this policy covers

This Privacy Policy applies to Phone Cleaner: Photo & Video, including the app's file processing, export, support, and purchase-related workflows.

Files and media processing

Phone Cleaner: Photo & Video works with Selected videos and photos that you choose. Processing is designed to happen locally on your device. Your selected files are not intentionally uploaded to Just Compress servers.

Data we do not intentionally collect

  • The contents of your selected files, documents, photos, videos, or audio recordings.
  • Your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, or advertising identifiers.
  • Account credentials, because the app does not require a Just Compress account.

Local app data

  • Recent local workflow state, output choices, and app preferences may be stored on your device.
  • Processed media is saved only when you choose to export or share it.

Permissions

  • Photo library access covers the library or selected items you authorize; file picker access covers files you choose. Local Network permission enables optional direct pairing.
  • Camera access is not required for normal conversion or compression workflows.

You can review or revoke app permissions in iOS Settings.

Third-party services

The app may use Apple frameworks and App Store services for file picking, media access, purchases, sharing, diagnostics, and distribution. We do not use advertising SDKs or sell personal data.

Mac and iPhone cleanup

Phone Cleaner is provided by Space Sheep Studio. Older versions may be named Video Compressor & MP4 Convert. Companion features require compatible versions on both devices, both apps open, and local-network access. Availability depends on the installed version.

When you choose paired cleanup, the Mac sends selected media filenames and aliases, capture dates, file sizes, media types and source asset identifiers directly to the paired iPhone. The iPhone returns request identifiers and deletion/review results. The local network link does not send the photo or video contents to the Mac, a developer server or a third-party AI service. A separate USB import you explicitly start copies media onto your Mac.

The phone displays matching local media for review. Ambiguous matches are excluded. Candidates with mismatched capture times require manual selection. Deletion requires your confirmation in the app and the Photos system prompt. Photos may synchronize deletions through iCloud Photos to your other devices. Items normally enter Recently Deleted; storage is not necessarily reclaimed immediately. Back up important originals before deleting and manage recovery through Photos.

This app cannot clear other apps’ caches or iOS system data. USB deletion is offered only when the connected device permits it; pairing does not bypass Photos permissions or confirmation. Importing or compressing a Mac copy alone does not remove the iPhone original.

If Mac pairing reports a network error, check System Settings > Privacy & Security > Local Network. On iPhone, check Settings > Privacy & Security > Local Network and Photos. Keep both apps in the foreground on the same local network. If a previously enabled Mac grant is not taking effect after a local development update, turn that app’s grant off and back on, then retry.

Pro access and billing

All Pro plans include unlimited exports, batch compression of up to 50 videos or 100 photos, conversion, splitting, speed changes and GIF creation. Monthly and yearly subscriptions provide the same Pro tools while active. Lifetime is a one-time purchase and does not renew. Monthly renews each month; yearly renews each year when offered through Apple. The purchase screen shows the total charge and billing period before confirmation. Manage or cancel subscriptions in Apple Account settings; cancellation stops the next renewal, with access continuing through the paid period. Apple processes payments.
