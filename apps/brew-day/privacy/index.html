Sober Wins for iPhone

Privacy Policy

Effective October 11, 2026 · Sober Wins 1.2 (formerly Brew Day Companion)

On-device records

Sober Wins, developed by Jiali Liang, stores your start dates, alcohol-free periods, daily drink/no-alcohol entries, collection points and reward claims, virtual collectibles, cabinet arrangements, settings and optional poster nickname on your iPhone. There is no app account, advertising, developer analytics or cloud sync. No sobriety history is uploaded to the developer or an AI service.

Optional Apple Health

With your permission, the app reads sleep and resting heart rate from Apple Health to compare with your daily log. Health samples are held in memory for on-device display. They are not uploaded, sold, used for advertising or included in shared posters. You can turn off Health trends in the app and manage access in Apple Health. Declining access leaves the tracker and collection available.

Optional camera mirror

The front camera can show a live mirror during a pause. Sober Wins does not take photos, record video or audio, analyze faces, or upload camera images. You can use breathing exercises without the camera.

Purchases, sharing and backups

Apple processes purchases and supplies entitlement status. We do not receive your payment card details. Apple services operate under Apple's policies. Saving or sharing exports only the image you choose to your Photos library or selected destination; that destination's practices apply. Device backups may include app data according to your Apple settings.

Your choices

You can correct daily records in History. Removing the app removes local records; save important exports first. Restoring a purchase restores Pro access, not deleted history or cabinet arrangements. Manage Apple backups and subscriptions separately in your Apple settings. Data from earlier Brew Day versions remains subject to on-device storage; this update does not upload it.

Support and this website

If you email support, we receive your address and information you choose to include, use them to respond, and do not use them for advertising. Request deletion of correspondence by contacting us. This site has no analytics scripts, advertising or forms. GitHub Pages may process ordinary network information to deliver it under GitHub's privacy statement. We will update this policy if practices change.
