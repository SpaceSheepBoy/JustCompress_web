What the app does

Track your alcohol-free days with Sober Wins. Log each day, keep your history, earn virtual beer collectibles, and arrange your fridge. Take a breathing pause when you need one.

Optional Apple Health access shows sleep and resting heart rate trends on your iPhone. These patterns are not medical advice or proof of cause.

Basic tracking, history, collectibles, the mini fridge and basic poster sharing are free. Pro adds premium nine-position cabinets, poster export and a 4× daily collection boost. Lifetime is a one-time purchase. Existing active Monthly subscriptions also provide Pro while valid; Lifetime does not cancel them. Virtual collectibles have no alcohol redemption or cash value.

Your tracking data stays on this device. No account is required.
