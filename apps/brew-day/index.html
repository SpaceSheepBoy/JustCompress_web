Homebrew Calculator: Brew Day

One place for your whole brew day.

Build recipes, correct gravity readings, run hop timers and keep a useful record of each batch on your iPhone.

View on the App Store

Build and reuse recipes

Create unlimited recipes with batch targets, fermentables, hops, yeast, other ingredients and ordered steps. Duplicate a recipe to make the next version while keeping the original.

Turn measurements into a next step

Use dilution and gravity adjustments, hydrometer temperature correction, fermented refractometer correction, Brix/SG conversion, strike-water temperature and estimated ABV. Check units and assumptions before applying a result.

Follow a batch from prep to packaging

Start from a recipe and keep its snapshot, completed steps, measurements, named timers and notes together. Hop additions use boil time remaining; local notifications and Live Activities help you follow the next action.

Keep a journal you can use again

Review past batches, repeat an existing plan, or export a complete summary and CSV. Recipes and records stay on your device unless you choose to share them.

Before you start

Recipes are always unlimited. Your first tracked brew is free without a subscription. Lifetime and Monthly unlock unlimited tracked sessions. Monthly auto-renews until cancelled. Calculations are estimates and do not establish fermentation completion or food safety.
