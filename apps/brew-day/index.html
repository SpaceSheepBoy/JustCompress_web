What you can do

  • Log alcohol-free days and review the local history.
  • Collect virtual items, arrange the mini fridge and take a breathing pause.
  • Optionally read sleep and resting-heart-rate trends from Apple Health.
  • Pro adds premium cabinets, poster export and a daily collection boost.

Access and purchases

Basic tracking, history, collectibles, the mini fridge and basic poster sharing are free. Lifetime is a one-time purchase. Existing active Monthly subscriptions remain Pro while valid; Lifetime does not cancel them.

Prices and availability can differ by storefront. Apple shows the current local offer before purchase.

Before you export or share

Health trends are not medical advice or proof of cause. Virtual collectibles have no cash or alcohol redemption value.
