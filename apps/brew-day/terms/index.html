Free features and virtual rewards

Basic daily tracking, history, earned collectibles, the three-position mini fridge, its poster export and basic progress sharing are available without Pro. The first daily record earns 24 collection points for either answer. Corrections do not award duplicate points. Alcohol-free days and streak rewards cannot be accelerated. Collectibles are virtual illustrations, with no cash value or alcohol redemption.

Pro and existing subscriptions

Pro Lifetime is a one-time purchase for premium nine-position cabinets, premium poster export and a 4× daily collection boost: 96 points instead of 24 for the first record of each day. The purchase screen shows the localized total price before Apple confirmation.

Existing active Monthly subscribers also receive Pro while their subscription is valid. Monthly automatically renews until canceled in Apple Account subscription settings. Buying Lifetime does not automatically cancel Monthly. Use Restore on the Pro screen with the Apple Account used for your purchase. Apple handles billing and refund requests.

Personal tracking

Sober Wins is a personal tracking and collection app. Breathing exercises and optional Health comparisons do not diagnose, treat or promise recovery from a medical condition. Health comparisons describe recorded observations, not proof of cause and effect. Local records are stored on this device; keep exports before removing the app.
