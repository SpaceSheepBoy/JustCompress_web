Sober Wins for iPhone

Support

Sober Wins helps you record alcohol-free days, log drinks, unlock virtual collectibles and arrange your collection.

Contact abel0911@icloud.com. Include the app version, iPhone model and issue description. Share only the information or screenshots you want us to review.

Daily tracking and rewards

Choose No alcohol or Had a drink to record your day. You can correct entries in History. Either answer earns daily collection points once per day: 24, or 96 with Pro. Collection rewards are virtual; they do not provide real alcohol. Editing an entry does not create another daily award.

Your collection

Open Collection, then My cabinet to arrange your earned items. The three-position mini fridge and its poster export are free. Pro adds premium nine-position cabinets, premium poster export and the daily collection boost. Pro does not accelerate sober milestones.

Pause and optional Health trends

Open Panic button for breathing and reflection tools. Camera access is optional and only provides a live mirror. Health trends optionally compares sleep and resting heart rate with your drink log. Enable its access through Apple Health; unavailable or denied data does not affect daily logging.

Purchases and your data

Use Restore on the Pro screen with the Apple Account used for your purchase. Existing active Monthly subscribers retain Pro. Cancel Monthly in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions; buying Lifetime does not cancel it automatically. Apple handles billing and refunds.

Records stay on this device. Restoring Pro does not recover deleted local history. Save important posters before removing the app. Sober Wins 1.2 replaces the former Brew Day experience.
