Brew Day Companion for iPhone

Support

Keep your brew measurements, timers, calculations and notes together on your iPhone.

For help, contact abel0911@icloud.com. Include the app version, your iPhone model and a description of the issue. Only share brew records or screenshots if you want us to review them.

Your first brew

One complete brew session is free. Starting a draft does not use the allowance; recording a measurement, starting a timer or saving a calculation does. The allowance does not start a paid subscription automatically.

Purchases and restoration

Pro unlocks unlimited brew sessions through a one-time Lifetime purchase or an automatically renewing Monthly subscription. Use Restore Purchases in the app while signed in to the Apple Account used for your purchase. Apple manages billing and refunds.

To cancel Monthly, open iPhone Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Purchasing Lifetime does not automatically cancel Monthly.

Timers and records

Enable notifications for timer alerts. Live Activities may show brew and timer names on your Lock Screen. Timers and calculations are aids; check your process and equipment independently.

Use the Journal to export a summary or CSV and delete individual brew records. Export important records before removing the app, because its records are stored locally.
