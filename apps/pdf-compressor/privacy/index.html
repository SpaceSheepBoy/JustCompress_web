Privacy Policy.

Privacy details for Just PDF Compressor. It covers the files you choose, app permissions, on-device data, purchases, and diagnostics.

PDF utility

Just PDF Compressor

Compress, sign, merge, convert JPG images to PDF, and export PDF pages as JPEG on iPhone and iPad.

SummaryJust PDF Compressor is designed to process PDF documents and selected JPG/JPEG images on your device. We do not intentionally collect your files, require an account, or use advertising trackers.

Effective date: May 15, 2026

What this policy covers

This Privacy Policy applies to Just PDF Compressor, including the app's file processing, export, support, and purchase-related workflows.

Files and media processing

Just PDF Compressor works with PDF documents and selected JPG/JPEG images that you choose. Processing is designed to happen locally on your device. Your selected files are not intentionally uploaded to Just Compress servers.

Data we do not intentionally collect

  • The contents of your selected files, documents, photos, videos, or audio recordings.
  • Your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, or advertising identifiers.
  • Account credentials, because the app does not require a Just Compress account.

Local app data

  • Recent local workflow state and app preferences may be stored on your device.
  • Output files are saved only when you choose to export or share them.

Permissions

  • Files access is limited to documents you choose.
  • Photo access is limited to selected images when you import photos for JPG to PDF workflows.

You can review or revoke app permissions in iOS Settings.

Purchases

Some advanced features may be offered through Apple in-app purchases. Apple processes payment and purchase history.

Diagnostics and App Store analytics

Apple may provide aggregated crash reports, performance information, and App Store analytics to developers. These reports depend on your Apple device settings and are used to improve reliability.

Third-party services

The app may use Apple frameworks and App Store services for file picking, media access, purchases, sharing, diagnostics, and distribution. We do not use advertising SDKs or sell personal data.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app changes. Material updates will be reflected on this page with a new effective date.
