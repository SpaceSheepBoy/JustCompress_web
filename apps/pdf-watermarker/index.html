What you can do

  • Select a PDF from Files and write your watermark text.
  • Choose repeated diagonal marks or a centered stamp.
  • Adjust text, opacity, size and color, and preview the pages.
  • Export a new marked copy while retaining the original.

Access and purchases

PDF Watermark Pro unlocks the export tools with lifetime or auto-renewing monthly access.

Prices and availability can differ by storefront. Apple shows the current local offer before purchase.

Before you export or share

A visible watermark identifies a copy. It does not encrypt the file or prevent every edit or redistribution.
