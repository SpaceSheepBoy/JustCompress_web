Useful answers

Before you start.

Is Apple Log the same as HDR?

No. Log footage and HDR video are different sources. VideoFix can convert supported Apple ProRes Log footage or HDR video to standard Rec.709 SDR color. The comparison on this page is an Apple Log example.

Can I convert HEVC that is already SDR?

Yes. Choose Convert HDR to SDR for an H.264 MP4 compatibility copy, including when the HEVC source is already SDR.

Will the copy look exactly like my original?

Conversion re-encodes the video, and HDR-to-SDR changes the brightness and color range. Keep your original and check color, sound and playback in the intended destination.

Can it fix every editing or playback problem?

No. A compatible codec or constant frame rate can help an appropriate workflow, but it cannot repair every existing synchronization problem. Device and destination requirements still apply.

Can I save only the sound?

Yes. Extract Audio saves the supported first audio track as an AAC M4A file.

What is included after the free conversions?

After three successful free conversions, Lifetime or Monthly Pro unlocks unlimited conversions. Lifetime is one payment; Monthly renews until you cancel it. Restore purchases with the Apple Account used for the purchase.
