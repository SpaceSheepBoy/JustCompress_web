GlowPop: Selfie Glow Camera

Light your next night selfie.

Turn your iPhone screen into soft light for selfies, front-camera videos and quick makeup checks.

View on the App Store

Start with white light

Adjust brightness and capture a selfie with the free white-light mode. Face the screen and move closer for stronger light; results depend on your surroundings.

Choose a color mood

Pro adds more colors, a custom color picker, light shapes, candle mode and RGB flow. Try warm light for a softer portrait or a colored glow for a different background mood.

Capture more than a still

Shoot photos, videos or Live Photos. Pro also includes retro filter saves. Save a result you like to Photos before sharing it.

A look inside the app

Before you start

White light, brightness control and selfie capture are free. Additional lighting modes and retro filter saves require Pro. Check the purchase screen for current local pricing.
