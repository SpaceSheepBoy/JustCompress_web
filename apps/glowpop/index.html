GlowPop: Selfie Glow Camera

Use screen light for selfies and video

US App Store version 1.0.3 · iOS 18.0 or later · Checked 2026-10-24

View on the App Store ↗

What you can do

  • Preview adjustable screen-light colors with the front camera open.
  • Adjust brightness and use the selfie mirror.
  • Capture photos and save them to the app album and Photos.
  • Pro includes unlimited photos and video recording.

Access and purchases

Preview light colors for free. The first 5 photos are free, including white-light photos. Lifetime or monthly Pro unlocks unlimited photos and video.

Prices and availability can differ by storefront. Apple shows the current local offer before purchase.

Before you export or share

Screen fill light changes the lighting around the front camera. Results depend on the device, distance and room light.
