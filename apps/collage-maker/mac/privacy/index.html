Privacy

Collage Maker · macOS · 2026-10-14

Your photos stay yours

Collage Maker processes your selected photos on your device. It does not upload your photos to a server, require an account, include advertising, or use third-party analytics.

Photo access

The system file dialog gives the app access only to images you choose. Photos stored in iCloud may be downloaded by Apple when you select them. Saving writes the finished image to your chosen location.

Storage and sharing

Imported copies are temporary and are removed when you start a new collage. Finished PNG/JPEG files are kept in the app's Documents/Collages folder. Copies saved to Photos or shared with another app are controlled by you and that service.

Purchases

Apple handles payments and purchase history. The app uses verified App Store transactions to check access, and stores whether your free export has been used on this device.
