What you can do

  • Combine 2 to 30 selected photos or screenshots.
  • Choose among 11 layouts, including grids and side-by-side arrangements.
  • Move and zoom photos, then export JPEG or PNG up to 3000 pixels wide.
  • Save to Photos, share with another app, or find the finished image in Files.

Access and purchases

One free export is included and every layout is available. Lifetime or monthly Pro unlocks unlimited exports.

Prices and availability can differ by storefront. Apple shows the current local offer before purchase.

Before you export or share

Processing stays on your device. The current store listing says no accounts, ads or watermarks.
