Useful answers

Before you start.

How many photos can I combine?

Select 2–30 photos or screenshots, then choose one of the 11 layouts.

Can I put two photos side by side?

Yes. Choose the side-by-side layout after selecting your images, then adjust each photo before exporting.

Can I make a long image from screenshots?

Yes. Choose the long-photo layout to place your selected screenshots vertically. Review the joins and framing before export.

What file can I save?

Export one JPEG or PNG image up to 3000 pixels wide. Save it to Photos, share it with another app, or find the finished image in Files.

Does it add a watermark?

The current app listing specifies no watermarks. One free export is included and every layout is available; Pro unlocks unlimited exports.

What is the difference between Lifetime and Monthly?

Lifetime is a one-time purchase for Pro access. Monthly is an auto-renewing subscription that continues until cancelled in your Apple Account. Check the local offer before purchasing.
