Dying Soon: Screen Time Limit

Make room for the time that matters.

Interrupt mindless scrolling with app blocking, screen time limits and a visual reminder that your day is finite.

View on the App Store

Put a pause before distracting apps

Choose apps to block and use limits and schedules to interrupt habitual scrolling. The memento mori block screen offers a moment to reconsider opening an app.

See time from a wider perspective

Explore your life grid in years, months or weeks. Use the countdown as a reflection exercise about limited time, not a prediction of your lifespan.

Return to a daily reminder

Collect reflections on mortality and choose how many daily reminders you receive, from zero to ten. Adjust the experience to the level of prompting that works for you.

Before you start

Download availability and any in-app purchase prices are shown in your local App Store. Screen time controls require the relevant iOS permissions. This is a focus and reflection tool, not a medical assessment or lifespan forecast.
