Cinomi Terms of Use

Cinomi — Cinema & Showtimes is provided by Space Sheep Studio. Updated November 17, 2026. The iOS app uses Apple’s Standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement (EULA), linked below.

Schedules and external booking

Schedules, availability, ratings, language and format information come from third parties and may change or be incomplete. Confirm details with the cinema. Cinomi does not sell cinema tickets; the cinema or booking service handles ticket purchases, refunds and its own terms.

Cinomi Pro subscriptions

From version 1.7.0, all cinema and film features are free with advertising. Cinomi Pro removes in-app advertising only; it does not unlock extra features. Earlier versions may offer different feature access. Pro is offered as monthly and yearly auto-renewable subscriptions. The app shows the available plan, duration, benefits and localized total charge before purchase. Payment is charged to your Apple Account. Subscriptions renew unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Manage or cancel through Apple’s subscription settings; cancellation stops future renewals and access normally continues through the paid period. Restore Purchases is available in the app. Introductory offers, if displayed, depend on Apple eligibility. We do not promise complete European cinema coverage.
