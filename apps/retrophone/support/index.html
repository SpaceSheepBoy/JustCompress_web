Y2K Cam: CCD Digital Camera

Turn your iPhone into a Y2K pocket camera with HoneyShot 7, Milky 55, DuskCam 5, and FlashPop 30 looks.

Need help?Email support@justcompress.online with the app name, iPhone or iPad model, iOS version, and what happened.

Quick checks

  • If the camera view does not appear, check Camera permission in iOS Settings.
  • If saving fails, check Photos permission and available device storage.
  • If capture is blocked after using your free allowance, check the remaining-shot counter. A verified Lifetime Unlock or active monthly subscription provides paid access.
  • If paid access does not activate after purchase, open the plan screen and tap Restore Purchases using the Apple Account used to buy it.
  • If a video has no sound, check Microphone permission in iOS Settings.

Paid access

Choose a one-time, non-consumable Lifetime Unlock or an auto-renewable monthly subscription. Both grant the same paid access while the entitlement is valid. The app displays the localized charge before purchase. Apple handles payments and purchase history. Manage or cancel a monthly subscription in your Apple Account settings; cancelling stops its next renewal, and access continues until the paid period ends.
