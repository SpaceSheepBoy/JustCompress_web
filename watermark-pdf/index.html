Help & FAQ

Watermark PDF FAQ

Short answers for common questions.

How do I add a watermark to a PDF, step by step?
  1. Choose a PDF.
  2. Type your watermark text.
  3. Pick placement and opacity.
  4. Click Add watermark.
What do the placement options do?

Tiled repeats across the page. Single adds one large stamp. Footer adds a small line at the bottom.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Your PDF stays on your device.

Does it add a watermark of its own?

Yes. The output includes your typed watermark plus a small Just Compress app logo watermark.
