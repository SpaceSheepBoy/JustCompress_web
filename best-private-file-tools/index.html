Steps, answers & details

Most people do not wake up looking for a "file toolkit." They have a practical problem: a PDF is too large for email, a form needs a signature, a recording is too long to send, or a document has to be turned into images.

  1. Private documents: contracts, IDs, invoices, bank statements, school forms, medical papers, and client PDFs.
  2. Private recordings: voice memos, interviews, meetings, unreleased audio, lectures, and podcasts.
  3. Private videos: family clips, personal recordings, and short videos you need to send.
