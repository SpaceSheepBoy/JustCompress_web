Privacy Policy

Updated October 30, 2026 · Hop Town · Space Sheep Studio

Hop Town is a virtual beer collection and puzzle game. This policy explains data used by the app, optional advertising, purchases, this website and support.

Your game progress

No game account is required. Your collection, coin balance, map progress, preferences and purchase delivery records are saved locally on your device. We do not upload your collection or game save to a developer-operated server. The app does not include third-party AI or a separate gameplay analytics service. A previous readable save may be retained locally for recovery. There is no developer-operated cloud sync. Deleting the app and its data can erase progress. Apple device backups and diagnostics depend on your Apple settings and Apple's policies.

Optional rewarded advertising

Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) supplies optional rewarded ads. The app requests applicable privacy information through Google User Messaging Platform (UMP), and uses an ad request flow when you choose a button offering an ad reward. Available rewards can include a revival, a clear action or additional coins. Tutorial rounds do not offer ad rewards. Rewards are granted only after the SDK reports that a reward was earned.

Google and its advertising partners may process IP addresses, approximate location inferred from IP, device and app information, identifiers, advertising views and interactions, and diagnostic or performance data to deliver ads, measure performance, prevent fraud and meet legal obligations. The app does not request precise location or send your beer collection or coin balance to the ad service. Ad personalization is disabled in this version. The app does not request Apple App Tracking Transparency permission or intentionally read the IDFA. These settings do not mean that advertising services process no data.

Where applicable, UMP provides choices about consent. You can review available choices through Advertising privacy choices in the app's settings. Ads may be unavailable depending on your choices, network or ad availability. You can continue playing without viewing rewarded ads. Google and its partners determine their retention and international processing under their policies and applicable law. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from partner apps.

Apple purchases

Apple processes optional coin packs and the starter offer through the App Store. We do not receive your payment card details. StoreKit verifies transactions; the app stores transaction identifiers, delivery and refund information locally to prevent duplicate delivery. Delivered consumable coins cannot be issued again by restoring purchases. Purchased coins do not expire. Deleting game data may erase your balance and collection.

This website and optional analytics

This website is hosted through GitHub Pages and Cloudflare. Hosting and network providers may process IP addresses, browser information and request logs to serve and secure pages. This is separate from the game save on your device.

The website loads a consent interface for optional PostHog analytics. Analytics starts only if you choose “Allow analytics” or previously allowed it on this website. You may choose “Reject” or change your choice using “Privacy choices.” The script respects supported Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals. Refusing website analytics does not affect the app.

If you allow analytics, PostHog receives page-visit and relevant App Store or download-link click events, browser/device context and technical connection information. Page addresses are reduced to their origin and path, excluding query strings and fragments. The site's configuration disables session recording, automatic event capture and person profiles. When enabled, PostHog uses browser local storage for analytics identifiers and related state. Your consent preference is also stored in browser local storage. You can clear this data in your browser settings. Analytics requests use PostHog’s US ingestion endpoint. We do not send game saves, uploaded files or form contents to this analytics service. For provider processing and retention, see PostHog's Privacy Policy and the JustCompress website privacy policy.

External links

Opening a brewery, support or policy link visits an external website. Those services process visits under their own policies.

Support and your choices

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any screenshots or device information you choose to provide. We use these to respond and resolve your request, and retain correspondence as needed for support and legal obligations. Do not send passwords or payment card details. You may contact us about access to or deletion of support correspondence. We cannot recover or delete your local game save from a server because we do not hold it.

You can avoid rewarded ads, manage applicable advertising choices in the app, reject or withdraw website analytics consent, avoid external links, and manage device backups or diagnostics in Apple settings. Deleting local app data may permanently remove progress.

Age and alcohol themes

Hop Town contains alcohol names and packaging and is intended for adults meeting applicable local age requirements. It is not directed at children, does not sell or deliver real alcohol, and does not require drinking alcohol. Rarity and prices are game design values.

Contact and updates

We may update this policy when features or data processing change. Privacy and support requests: support@justcompress.online.
