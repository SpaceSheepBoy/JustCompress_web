Hop Town support

Email support@justcompress.online with the game version, your iPhone model and the steps leading up to the problem. A screenshot can help. Omit payment details and passwords.

Collect and display beers

Match three of the same food tile to earn coins. A run has 18 stages. Map pieces can be earned starting at stage three. Spend coins on a specific virtual beer in a town shop, then place it in your fridge. Hop Town has five towns and 313 collectibles. Each cabinet displays 3, 6 or 9 items, depending on its size. Your other beers stay in your collection.

Save or share a fridge snapshot

Open the snapshot in the fridge and choose a format. Save Image asks for permission to add the picture to Photos. You can also use Share to choose a destination yourself.

Find a missing purchase

Open the coin shop with the + beside your balance and choose the option to check purchases. The shop checks with Apple for unfinished transactions. Already delivered consumables cannot be restored a second time. Prices are shown in your local currency when the App Store loads them. You can also earn coins by playing.

Recover local progress

Keep the app installed if a purchase or save is missing. Progress is stored on this device. If the main save is unreadable, the game tries its local backup. If both copies are unreadable, the game pauses to protect them. Contact support with the message you see. Recovery through a device backup depends on your Apple settings.

Optional rewards and ad reports

Tutorial rounds have no rewarded ads. In eligible rounds you can choose an optional reward such as a revival or extra coins. To report an inappropriate ad, email support with the time you saw it and a short description. Attach a screenshot only if you want to share it.

Sound and motion

Check the sound control in Settings, the iPhone volume and the current audio output. The game supports the system Reduce Motion preference.

酒花小镇游戏帮助

三张相同小食牌可以消除。每轮共 18 关，第 3 关起可获得地图碎片。五个小镇共有 313 款虚拟啤酒。用金币在商店挑选藏品，回到冰箱陈列。不同尺寸的冰箱可摆 3、6 或 9 件，其他藏品保留在收藏里。

需要保存冰箱快照时，选择图片尺寸并允许添加到照片，也可点“分享”自行选择去向。金币购买未到账时，点余额旁的加号，再点“检查未到账购买”。已经交付的消耗品无法重复恢复。请保留游戏和本机存档。

如果提示存档损坏，游戏会尝试读取本地备份。两份都无法读取时会暂停，避免覆盖。遇到存档、购买或广告问题，请把游戏版本、设备型号和出现问题的步骤发给支持团队。举报广告时可提供出现时间和说明，截图由你决定是否附上。请勿发送密码或支付信息。
