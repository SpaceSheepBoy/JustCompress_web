How to extract audio from a video on iPhone

Keep the sound without the picture. This walkthrough saves a video’s audio as a separate M4A file.

The quick route: open Audio Compressor MP3 Converter → Extract from Video → choose a clip → extract → Save audio.

From video to an audio file

From our recorded App walkthrough. Tap either image to enlarge. File sizes shown belong to this example; your result will vary. The interface may differ in newer versions.

Extract the sound in 3 steps

  1. Choose the clip. In Audio Compressor MP3 Converter, tap Extract from Video and select your video.
  2. Start extraction. Tap Extract Audio from Video. Keep the App open while it processes.
  3. Save and listen. Tap Save audio, choose a location, then play the saved file to check the sound.

Need MP3 instead? The walkthrough exports M4A. Convert the saved M4A to MP3 if your destination requires it.

No sound in the result?

Play the original video with its volume up. A clip without an audio track has no sound to extract. Extraction keeps the existing soundtrack; it does not transcribe speech or separate a voice from background music.
