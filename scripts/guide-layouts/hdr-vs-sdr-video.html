HDR vs SDR,
explained visually.

HDR has room for brighter highlights. SDR is often easier to share. Here is when to keep each.

The differenceShould I convert?How to convert

01. Same scene. Different brightness range.

SDR

Standard dynamic range

A conventional delivery format for everyday playback. Rec.709 is a common SDR color standard.

HDR

High dynamic range

More room for bright reflections and light sources. The display and playback app must handle it correctly.

Definitions: Apple’s video format guide and HDR display explanation.

SDR does not mean blurry.

Dynamic range and resolution are different. An SDR video can still be 4K.
