How to combine photos into one image on iPhone

Send one picture instead of several. Pick your photos, choose a layout, then save the finished collage.

Two photos? Choose a side-by-side layout. For three or more, use a grid and check the crop before exporting.

Three photos, one picture

Make your own in 3 steps

  1. Select your photos. Open Combine Photos: Stitch & Grid and choose the pictures you want together.
  2. Choose the layout. Use a pair for two pictures or a grid for a collection. Drag or zoom each photo so faces and important details stay visible.
  3. Export and check. Save to Photos or Files. Open the saved image, check the edges, then share it.

Keep the original photos too. A collage arranges them in one image; it does not blend them or automatically remove overlapping screenshot content.

Keep the photos readable

For a comparison, keep the subjects at a similar size and in a clear order. If text is too small, try fewer photos or a vertical pair. Adjust any cut-off edges before exporting, then open the saved copy at normal phone size.

Prefer to do it in your browser?

Open the free photo combiner for a one-off collage without installing the App. Use fewer photos if text becomes too small to read.
