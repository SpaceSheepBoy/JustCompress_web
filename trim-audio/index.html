❓ Quick answers
Can I trim audio without uploading?

Yes. Your audio stays on your device. It is not uploaded.

What do start and end seconds mean?

Start is where the kept section begins. End is where it stops. For example, start 10 and end 45 keeps the section from 10 seconds to 45 seconds.
