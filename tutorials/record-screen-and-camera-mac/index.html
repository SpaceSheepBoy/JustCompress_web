CamRecorder for Mac / Screen and camera tutorial

MAC TUTORIAL · FOUR STEPS

Record your screen.
Keep yourself in the picture.

Walk through an app, explain a presentation, or teach a workflow. Capture your screen and camera together with CamRecorder.

macOS 14 or later · 60 minutes of free recording in total
01 Choose a screen02 Set up your camera03 Start recording04 Find your video
01

Choose a screen or window

Under Recording mode, select Full Screen + Camera. Click Choose Screen… and select a screen in the macOS picker.

To show just one app, choose Window + Camera. If a source is already selected, the button reads Change Screen….

Close private windows and silence notifications before recording.
02

Set up your camera and placement

Choose a camera under Camera, such as MacBook Pro Camera. Use Camera placement to choose a corner that keeps important screen content visible.

Check your lighting and framing in the preview. Select your microphone and watch the level indicator while speaking. If you need computer audio, check that source too.

03

Check the preview, then record

Choose a save folder on first use and grant the recording permissions requested by macOS. Check your screen, camera, and audio sources, then click Record video.

When you finish, click Stop & save to stop recording and save the file.

Make a short test recording first. Play it back to check your audio and camera placement.
04

Find and play your video

Click Saved · Show in Finder to locate the saved file. Open it and check the screen, camera, and audio before sharing.

To choose a different save folder, use Change… in the save area.

Before you share

Screen text is readable, and your camera does not cover important controls.

Your voice is clear, and any computer audio you need is included.

No private notifications, account details, or unintended windows are visible.

Record your first walkthrough.

Basic recording works without Plus effects. Free recording includes 60 minutes in total, not a daily allowance. Plus is a one-time purchase for unlimited recording, background blur, and Screen Light. Check the Mac App Store for your local price.
