Burn Captions into an iPhone Video for Reliable Sharing

Export a captioned MP4 or MOV when you want words to stay visible as part of a shared video. An SRT file is separate timed text and will only appear when the receiving player or platform loads it correctly.

Choose the approach

Choose permanent or editable

Burned-in captions become video pixels. They travel with that export but cannot be switched off or restyled as a separate subtitle track. Keep an uncaptioned original and an editable transcript when you need later changes.

Try it on iPhone

  1. Import your spoken video into Subtitle Maker: Just Captions and generate captions. Correct names, words and timing before styling.
  2. Choose a readable caption style and preview the full frame, including faces and any existing lower-third text.
  3. Export a captioned MP4 or MOV. Open the saved video outside the editor and test the exact file you plan to send.

Preview is not the delivery file

A caption visible in an editing preview does not establish that you selected a captioned-video export. Check a few points in the saved clip with audio muted, especially after cuts. Sending the original video plus an SRT is a different workflow and may need separate import at the destination.

Know the app options

Our iPhone app provides captioned video and SRT exports. Free single-video exports include a Just Captions watermark; Pro removes it and unlocks additional features. Core captions can run on device; optional online AI text tools require consent. Space Sheep Studio develops Just Captions. This guide covers adding captions from speech, not extracting or removing words already burned into someone else’s video.
