iPhone Video Audio Drifts Out of Sync After Import?

Compare the first and last visible sound cues. If only the editor drifts, test one constant-frame-rate export against both cues.

Quick check

Check two audible events

Look for a clap, a drum hit or a visible consonant near each end. If the original camera audio lines up early but drifts late, check how your editor handles the clip’s timestamps. Test with camera audio alone first.

Try it on iPhone

  1. Play the source in Photos and compare an early and a late cue.
  2. In VideoFix, make one steady frame-rate copy. Choose the frame rate appropriate for your timeline.
  3. Import both files into a fresh timeline. Compare both cues before processing the rest.

If the clip came from an HDR recording

Check color separately if the source is HDR. Use the clap to compare sync first. If your project needs HDR delivery, verify the color of any test export before you replace source media.

Common questions

Will steady frame rate fix every sync issue?

No. It tests one source of drift. If both files drift, check the editor settings or the source recording.

What if sound is late by the same amount throughout?

That is a fixed offset. Align one clear cue and confirm a second cue before re-encoding.
