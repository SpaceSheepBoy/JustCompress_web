Mark a PDF CONFIDENTIAL: A Watermark Is Not Redaction

Add a visible CONFIDENTIAL watermark to label a PDF copy, while keeping required content readable. The mark does not remove private information: underlying text can remain accessible. Use a separate, verified redaction workflow when information must actually be removed.

Choose the approach

Label versus remove

A watermark communicates handling intent. Redaction removes selected content from the deliverable. A pale stamp, black-looking overlay or flattened preview should not be treated as proof that the underlying text and metadata are gone.

Try it on iPhone

  1. Decide whether the recipient needs a labeled complete document or a copy with information removed. Confirm their requirements first.
  2. For labeling only, open the PDF in PDF Watermark & Stamp, enter CONFIDENTIAL or an appropriate purpose, and preview the pages.
  3. Export the new marked copy and inspect it outside the app. Keep the original separate and verify that the label has not covered required content.

What the watermark does not prove

A visible mark is not encryption, an access permission, a digital signature or a reliable barrier against copying. It also cannot establish who is authorized to receive the document. Avoid putting a sensitive account number into the watermark itself: the mark becomes another visible copy of that information.
