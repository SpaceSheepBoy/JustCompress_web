Remove Location from a Photo Copy Before Sharing on iPhone

To remove location from a photo you share, export a separate copy with location retention disabled, then inspect the exported file’s metadata. This removes a metadata clue; visible landmarks and other details in the picture can still reveal where it was taken.

Choose the approach

Check the file you will send

The original in Photos and an exported copy can have different metadata. Inspect the exact file attached to your message or upload, rather than assuming a setting changed every copy already in another app.

Try it on iPhone

  1. Open the photo in PhotoFix: HDR to Normal Photos on iPhone and choose an appropriate supported conversion.
  2. Disable location retention for the export. Decide separately whether to retain capture date and keep the original untouched.
  3. Save a new copy, inspect its location information in a trusted photo/file inspector, and share that verified file.

Native sharing is another option

Apple also offers location controls when sharing photos. Check the available Options in the share sheet for your iOS version and destination. A recipient may still infer location from signs, buildings or faces. Metadata removal is therefore a useful file-handling step rather than an anonymity guarantee.

Be aware of conversion changes

Our app supports choosing whether to keep capture date and location, but conversion can also change format or presentation. JPEG uses compression and replaces transparency with white; choose a supported output that suits the recipient. This guide makes no claim about stripping every possible private field or altering copies already shared.
