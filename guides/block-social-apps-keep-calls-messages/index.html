Quick check

List your essential apps

Decide which apps you need during the block. Include Phone, Messages, transport or a work authenticator as applicable. Leave them out of the block. Check each member before selecting a whole category.

Try it on iPhone

  1. In Dying Soon, choose the distracting apps individually.
  2. Start a thirty-minute Block Now session. Try one blocked app and each essential app. Make a test call or message if those need to work.
  3. Adjust the selection before relying on a recurring schedule. Test again if you change your routine or install an app that you need.

Check what manages availability

Keep your existing Focus preferences in mind during the test. If one app remains closed or silent after your test block ends, review other restrictions that were already active.

Common questions

What if an app opens but its alerts are gone?

Review Focus and Screen Time separately. An available app can still have notifications silenced.

What if I block an app by mistake?

Check temporary unlock options and correct the selection for the next session.
