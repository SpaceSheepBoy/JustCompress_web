Take a Short App Break Without Ending Your Blocking Routine

For a short break from blocked apps, choose an available temporary unlock and check the blocking state again when the break ends. A bounded pause is easier to review when you decide what you need before opening the app.

Choose the approach

Give the break one purpose

Write down the message or task you intend to handle. Choose the shortest available period that fits it. A timer does not stop a feed from being tempting, so leave the app when the task is finished rather than trying to use every remaining minute.

Try it on iPhone

  1. In Dying Soon: Screen Time Limit, keep your selected app-blocking routine active and use its temporary-unlock option when needed.
  2. Choose an available five-, fifteen- or thirty-minute pause. Complete the intended task and close the distracting app.
  3. After the selected interval, try a selected app and confirm the expected blocking state. Review Screen Time authorization and your active routine if it is not as expected.

Check the actual boundary

A temporary unlock is deliberate access, not a guarantee against bypassing settings. Blocking depends on the selected apps, current permissions and active session or schedule. Test the boundary before relying on it during an important work period.

Keep routines simple

Space Sheep Studio develops Dying Soon for iPhone. One scheduled rule and unlimited thirty-minute Block Now sessions are free; Pro adds unlimited scheduled rules. Temporary unlocks are listed as five, fifteen or thirty minutes. Notifications are optional and are not a substitute for checking the blocking state. The life grid is a reflection tool, not a medical prediction. This workflow makes no promise about productivity or a guaranteed reduction in screen time.
