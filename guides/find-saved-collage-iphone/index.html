Saved a Collage but Cannot Find It? Check Photos and Files

Check where you sent the collage. Look in Photos if you saved it there, or return to the folder and storage provider you selected in Files.

Trace the export destination

Start with the decision

An export preview, a completed save and sharing to another app are different steps. Before recreating the collage or paying for another export, identify the destination you chose and look for the existing JPEG or PNG.

Find and open the saved image

  1. If you chose Photos, open Photos and inspect recently saved items. If the app showed a save error or a permission request, resolve that specific message rather than assuming an export exists.
  2. If you chose Files, open Files, use Browse and check the folder under On My iPhone, iCloud Drive or the provider you selected. Search by the filename if you remember it.
  3. Open the found image and verify the arrangement. To make a photo-library copy from a supported image in Files, use its share options and Save Image when offered. Keep the file you already found.

Avoid guessing an album or filename

Apple’s Files guidance distinguishes device storage, iCloud Drive and other providers. Downloads is not the destination for every save. A share action can also hand an image to another app without placing it in the folder you expected. Note the exact destination on your next export and open that copy immediately.

What Combine Photos saves

It combines 2 to 30 images and exports JPEG or PNG to Photos, Files or another app. One export is free, with Pro required for further exports. No automatic recovery of an unsaved composition or universal collage album is promised. Once you locate the image, inspect its crop and readability before sharing it.

Common questions

I tapped Share. Does that always save a copy in Photos?

No. The destination may receive the image without adding it to your library. Check where the share action sent it.

Should I start over if the finished collage is missing?

First look for an existing copy in the location you chose. If you export again from an available project, pick a known folder and open the saved file immediately.
