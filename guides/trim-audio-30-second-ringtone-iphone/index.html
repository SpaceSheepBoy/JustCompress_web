Choose a clear excerpt, trim it to 30 seconds or less, and export a ringtone-ready copy. Then install that copy using GarageBand.

Quick check

Cut the hook before installing it

Start the clip where you want the sound to begin every time the phone rings. Leave enough room at the end for the musical phrase or spoken word to finish.

Try it on iPhone

  1. Save the audio you have permission to use in Files. Keep an untouched version so you can try another cut later.
  2. Open it in Audio Compressor MP3 Converter and select its trim tool. Preview the beginning and the end of the excerpt. Cut to 30 seconds or less for the Apple ringtone workflow.
  3. Export a separate ringtone-ready copy. Open GarageBand, import the copy into an Audio Recorder project and follow Apple’s Share > Ringtone steps. Once exported, choose how to use the ringtone.

Make the first second useful

A long lead-in or a very quiet start may make the ringtone hard to notice. Preview the excerpt from silence at normal phone volume. If the opening needs adjustment, return to the original recording for a new cut.

Check the duration before you share

GarageBand can shorten a long clip to fit. Making the trim yourself gives you control over the endpoint. Some protected music files cannot be imported. Start with a local file you are allowed to use. Check the current export access options in the app.

Common questions

Can I keep two versions?

Yes. Export each excerpt under a different name so you can tell them apart when you install them.

Do I need the whole song in GarageBand?

You can import your prepared excerpt. Keep the longer recording separately for future edits.
