Block Distracting Apps at Bedtime on iPhone

Use a recurring app-blocking schedule to restrict selected distractions at bedtime. A daily usage allowance answers a different question: how long you can use an app, rather than which hours it should be unavailable.

Choose the approach

Start with one routine

Choose the apps that keep you scrolling and a realistic bedtime window. Keep essential communication and any overnight tasks in mind before selecting what to restrict.

Try it on iPhone

  1. In Dying Soon: Screen Time Limit, grant the requested Apple Screen Time access and choose the distracting apps.
  2. Create a recurring schedule for your bedtime routine. Check its selected days and start/end times.
  3. Test the selected apps during the window and review the next morning. Adjust the routine if it blocks something you need.

Understand the available exits

The app offers temporary unlocks of 5, 15 or 30 minutes. A schedule is therefore a practical friction tool, not a claim that every bypass is impossible. Its separate Block Now feature starts a 30-minute focus session; use that for a one-off break rather than treating it as the nightly recurring rule.

Native option and app limits

Apple Screen Time also offers scheduled Downtime; check its blocking setting rather than assuming a reminder prevents continued use. Its optional life grid uses your chosen birthday and lifespan setting for reflection, not a health forecast. Notifications are optional and are not needed to define this bedtime routine.
