Scan a Receipt to PDF on iPhone Without Cutting Off the Total

To scan a receipt into a useful PDF, capture the whole sheet and check the merchant, date, line items and total before saving. A PDF container cannot recover an edge that was left outside the camera frame.

Choose the approach

Start with the decision

Lay the receipt on a flat, contrasting surface with even light. Smooth a curled edge gently and keep fingers and hard shadows away from the text. If the paper is faded, preserve the best original capture rather than expecting compression to reconstruct it.

Try it on iPhone

  1. Open Scan to PDF in PDF Compressor: Sign & Merge and frame the receipt with all edges visible.
  2. Review the captured page before saving. If the bottom, date or an item is cut off or blurred, capture it again. Use the available review controls without assuming a particular crop interface.
  3. Save the PDF, reopen it and zoom in on the smallest important text. Check the actual file before uploading it to an expense or reimbursement system.

Capture comes before compression

A sharp capture gives later compression more to work with. Avoid reducing the file until the total and fine text are readable. If the destination needs a smaller PDF, make a separate compressed copy and inspect it again. A photographic PDF is not automatically searchable text or a verified expense record.
