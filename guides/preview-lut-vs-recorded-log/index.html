Preview LUT or Baked Look? Check What the Camera Recorded

A preview LUT can change how footage looks without changing the recording. Check the camera settings and the original clip before assuming a look was saved into the picture.

Check where the look is applied

Start with the distinction

A camera app may show a pleasing Rec.709 view while retaining Log footage for later processing. Apple’s Final Cut Camera documentation describes a separate Preview with LUT option. Other camera apps can also offer recording options, so inspect the actual setting and saved clip.

Compare the original with and without the preview

  1. Keep the untouched recording and note the camera app, recording profile and whether a look was set to preview only or recorded into the clip.
  2. Open a short saved clip in the intended editor. Check for an automatically applied camera transform before deciding that a flat or contrasty picture is the recorded appearance.
  3. If you have supported Apple ProRes Log footage and need a standard-color sharing file, export a separate Rec.709 SDR H.264 MP4 with VideoFix. Inspect that saved copy in the destination player.

Stored pixels and viewing choices differ

An editor can interpret Log and apply a viewing transform on import. Turning that transform off changes the preview without rewriting the source. A look already rendered into picture pixels cannot generally be removed by toggling a viewer LUT. Retaining the Log original keeps options open; do not judge the file solely by a thumbnail.

Make a deliberate delivery copy

Its current public listing supports Apple ProRes Log to Rec.709 conversion, with three conversions free before an unlock is required. This guide does not establish support for Apple Log 2, ProRes RAW or arbitrary camera profiles. Conversion makes a new delivery file; it does not reverse a previously baked grade.

Common questions

Can the thumbnail prove that a LUT was baked in?

No. Thumbnails can use their own interpretation or preview settings. Check an original frame with the viewing transform turned off in a trusted editor.

Can I turn off a look that was rendered into the file?

Turning off a viewer LUT can only remove a viewing transform. Use the original Log file if you need to grade without a look already rendered into the pixels.
