Before-and-After Photos Confusing? Keep the Order Consistent

For a readable before-and-after collage, put the earlier image first and the later image second, then state the order and dates in your accompanying caption. Consistent framing helps viewers compare the actual subject.

Choose the approach

Decide what is comparable

Choose photos of the same subject and, where possible, the same angle and distance. A changed crop, light or pose can exaggerate a difference. Keep the originals so viewers can inspect context when that matters.

Try it on iPhone

  1. Select the two photos in Combine Photos: Stitch & Grid and choose a side-by-side or suitable stacked arrangement.
  2. Check the sequence in the preview. Move and zoom each photo so the subject has a comparable size without stretching it.
  3. Export JPEG or PNG and inspect the saved collage. Add the dates and “earlier first, later second” in the message or post that accompanies it.

Make the comparison easy on phones

A narrow screen can hide small details in a horizontal pair. Choose a taller arrangement when both subjects need more width. Inspect the actual shared copy because messaging and social destinations may resize images. Text in an accompanying caption may be separated from the image when forwarded.
