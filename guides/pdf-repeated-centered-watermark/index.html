Repeated or Centered PDF Watermark? Choose the Coverage

Choose a repeated PDF watermark when the purpose mark should appear across the page; choose one centered mark when a simple document-status stamp is enough. Preview the actual document because coverage and readability compete.

Choose the approach

Start with the decision

A short word such as DRAFT can work as one centered stamp. A repeated purpose mark spreads the wording over more of the sheet. Neither layout prevents someone from copying, editing or photographing the document, and neither replaces redaction.

Try it on iPhone

  1. Keep the unmarked original. Open a copy from Files in PDF Watermark & Stamp and enter short, specific wording.
  2. Choose the repeated diagonal or single centered style. Adjust available size, color and opacity while reviewing the real page content.
  3. Export a new PDF and inspect dense text, signatures and pages with different orientations. Send the checked marked copy, not the original by mistake.

Coverage is not security

A repeated pattern may remain visible in more cropped portions of a page than a single stamp. That is an observation about placement, not tamper resistance. Do not cover details the recipient must read, or assume a purpose statement controls what happens to the file. Confirm the recipient accepts visible marks before preparing a sensitive application.

Use the supported modes
