Crop Repeated Headers Before Stitching Screenshots

Crop away the repeated header and use one shared line to decide where each screenshot ends. Then stack the cropped screenshots in reading order.

Quick check

Pick a line you can find twice

In two overlapping screenshots, find a distinctive sentence, message or list item. Keep it at the end of the first image. Start the second image just below it.

Try it on iPhone

  1. Duplicate the captures if you want separate working copies. In Photos, crop the fixed header or footer from each image where it repeats. Leave the first header if it tells the reader what the screen is about.
  2. Crop the join at a gap between two lines. Check that the first screenshot ends on the last complete line you want to keep and the second starts on the next one. Use the same left and right edges.
  3. Place the crops in order in the vertical layout in Combine Photos: Stitch & Grid. Export one image. Zoom in on the join and check that no line repeats or disappears.

Keep the next capture easy to join

When you take new screenshots, scroll far enough to show new content but keep a few lines from the earlier image. Hold the phone orientation and text size steady. Choose an item that appears only once as your landmark.

Check the shared copy
