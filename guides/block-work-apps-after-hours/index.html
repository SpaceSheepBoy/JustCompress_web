Keep Work Apps Closed After Hours on iPhone

To put work apps away after hours, select those apps and create an off-hours blocking schedule while keeping essential access available. Check the boundary at both its start and end before relying on it.

Choose the approach

Choose the boundary first

Decide which work apps can wait and which you may need for urgent contact. Blocking an app differs from silencing its notifications. A browser or another device may still provide access, so define a realistic phone routine rather than assuming an organization-wide lock.

Try it on iPhone

  1. In Dying Soon: Screen Time Limit, authorize Screen Time access and select the work apps you want to block.
  2. Set the available recurring schedule for your intended off-hours period. Review the selected days and times; use additional rules only where your plan supports them.
  3. Test a selected app while the rule is active and again after it ends. Check that essential communication remains available.

Handle multiple routines explicitly

An evening rule and a separate weekend routine may need different schedules. Avoid unintended overlaps and inspect each rule rather than assuming one covers every day correctly. Temporary unlocks are available for deliberate access, but are not a guarantee against bypassing settings.

Check the free and Pro scope

Space Sheep Studio develops Dying Soon for iPhone. One scheduled rule and unlimited thirty-minute Block Now sessions are free; Pro adds unlimited scheduled rules. Its temporary unlocks offer five, fifteen or thirty minutes. Blocking depends on current permissions and active selections. Notifications are optional, and the life grid is a reflection tool rather than a prediction. This routine does not promise perfect enforcement or a particular productivity result.
