Quick check

Find a key that fits

To shift G major down to F major, count two semitones: G to F-sharp to F. The old G–C–D chords become F–B-flat–C. Sing the high phrase first, then the lowest phrase to check the whole range.

Try it on iPhone

  1. Open a local copy in Audio Compressor MP3 Converter.
  2. Lower the pitch by two semitones if the control shows semitones. Leave tempo at its starting value.
  3. Check the first chord against a keyboard, then export and name your practice copy with the new key.

Check the whole phrase

Listen for the point where the key change feels most useful. A lead note can guide a quick pitch check, but the surrounding chords tell you whether the backing track fits the whole arrangement. Keep a normal-pitch copy for playing with the original recording.
