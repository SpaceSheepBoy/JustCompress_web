Collage or Carousel? Decide How the Photos Should Be Read

Use a collage when readers need to see the photos together; use a carousel when each photo needs its own viewing space. A saved collage is one image, so its individual panels cannot become separate slides automatically.

Choose the approach

Start with the decision

A pair of design options benefits from a single glance. A sequence of detailed travel photos may benefit from separate files. Decide what the reader should compare before choosing a posting format, rather than assuming one format gets more reach.

Try it on iPhone

  1. Choose two or a few photos whose relationship matters when viewed together. Keep each original for other uses.
  2. In Combine Photos: Stitch & Grid, select the pictures and choose a layout. Move and zoom each image so the important subject stays visible.
  3. Export JPEG or PNG and inspect the saved image at its intended viewing size. If every photo needs more room, share the originals as separate images using the destination’s own controls.

One file changes the experience

A collage travels as a single attachment and preserves your arrangement even in a simple message. The tradeoff is that each panel gets only part of the total pixel area. A carousel keeps files separate and depends on the receiving service’s presentation. Check its current upload requirements independently.
