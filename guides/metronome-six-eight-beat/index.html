Metronome Too Slow in 6/8? Check Which Note Gets the Click

A metronome can feel three times too slow in 6/8 when its clicks count dotted quarters but you expect eighth notes. Read the note symbol beside the tempo number before changing BPM.

Choose the approach

Two groups of three

A common 6/8 feel has two large beats per bar, each containing three eighth notes. If the marking is dotted quarter = 60, there are 60 large beats or 180 eighth-note subdivisions per minute. Setting a plain click to 60 and counting six clicks per bar changes the intended speed.

Try it on iPhone

  1. Read the score’s complete tempo marking. Do not infer the beat unit from the number 6 alone.
  2. Use Audio Compressor MP3 Converter’s metronome as a steady click reference. For dotted quarter = 60, count “ONE two three FOUR five six” over two clicks.
  3. If you need each eighth note sounded instead, use 180 clicks per minute where the available range permits. Check against the same phrase before practicing.

Let the score decide

Some slow passages mark eighth notes explicitly. In that case follow that marking rather than multiplying automatically. Counting aloud can establish the grouping without assuming the app offers a dedicated compound-meter or subdivision mode.
