Export SRT Subtitles on iPhone for an Editable Caption Track

Export SRT when you need subtitles as an editable timed-text file separate from the video. Generate captions, correct the words and timing, then save the SRT with its matching video. Export a captioned MP4 or MOV when you need the text permanently visible instead.

Choose the approach

SRT is a sidecar

An SRT contains caption text and start/end times. It does not contain your video. YouTube supports basic UTF-8 SRT but does not recognize styling markup, so do not expect animated word highlights to transfer.

Try it on iPhone

  1. Import the spoken video into Subtitle Maker: Just Captions and generate captions on iPhone.
  2. Review names, punctuation and timing against the video. Complete any video trims before the final caption check.
  3. Choose SRT export and save the file beside the matching video. Import both into the receiving editor or platform and preview the beginning and end.

Check synchronization after editing

A subtitle file can be correct for one cut and wrong for another. Removing an introduction, changing playback speed or moving a scene can shift later captions. Keep a clear shared filename for the video and subtitle version. Check several points through a long clip rather than only the first line.

Choose the right export

Our app offers SRT text and captioned MP4/MOV exports. Core caption generation can run on device; optional online AI text tools require consent. A separate SRT lets the destination render captions, while a captioned video carries their visible appearance. Free single-video exports include a Just Captions watermark; Pro unlocks further features.
