Can’t Follow a Metronome? Start with One Short Phrase

If you cannot follow a metronome, start by tapping with its clicks, then play one short phrase at a manageable tempo. Adding the whole song immediately gives you more things to coordinate than listening to the pulse alone.

Choose the approach

Make the task smaller

Choose a phrase you already know. Listen for a few beats, tap along, then say or clap the rhythm before playing. If you repeatedly rush or stop, simplify the phrase before increasing speed.

Try it on iPhone

  1. Open the metronome tool in Audio Compressor MP3 Converter and choose a comfortable BPM using the available controls.
  2. Tap with the pulse first. Play one short phrase while keeping the clicks audible. Stop and restart deliberately when the pulse gets lost.
  3. Write down the tempo at which the phrase stays steady. Increase gradually only when several repetitions feel controlled.

A click is a reference

BPM describes beats per minute; it does not decide which note value you should count. Use the pulse indicated by your score or teacher. Avoid changing the beat interpretation and speed at the same time, since you may accidentally make the task twice as fast.
