Screen Light Reflecting in Your Glasses? Adjust the Selfie Angle

If screen light reflects in your glasses, slightly change the phone and face angle, reduce brightness and check a saved selfie. With screen fill, camera and light move together, so changing one angle can affect framing as well as reflection.

Choose the approach

Find the reflection path

Look for a bright screen-shaped patch over the eyes. A small movement may shift it, but a different lens coating, screen position or ambient lamp can change the result. Do not expect one universal angle to remove every reflection.

Try it on iPhone

  1. Open GlowPop: Selfie Glow Camera with a neutral screen color and moderate brightness. Keep the whole face in frame.
  2. Move the phone slightly above or to one side and turn your face a little. Watch the eyes, framing and facial shadows together.
  3. Take a photo and inspect the saved result. Try a small brightness or angle change if a screen reflection still covers the eyes.

Use the room light too

Soft light from a nearby window or lamp can reduce dependence on a bright screen. Check whether another light is producing the glare before changing the phone repeatedly. Avoid uncomfortable brightness. This is a lighting adjustment, not a digital glare-removal or eyeglass-retouching feature.
