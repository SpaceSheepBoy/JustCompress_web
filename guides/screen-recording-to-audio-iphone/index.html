Quick check

Check which sound was captured

A screen recording can contain app sound, microphone sound, both, or neither. Play the source through the section you need before exporting. Some apps restrict recording.

Try it on iPhone

  1. Find the original screen recording in Photos. Turn up playback volume and listen to the section you want. Keep the video so you can retry from the source.
  2. Open the video in Audio Compressor MP3 Converter and use the audio extraction tool. Choose an available audio format the recipient can open and save the output as a separate file.
  3. Play the exported audio from Files. Check the beginning, the end and any quiet sections that matter. Give the file a name that identifies the recording without opening it.

If the original is silent

Check the video in another player and make sure audio playback is on. An extraction tool can save sound from an existing track. If your microphone or the app audio was never recorded, make a new recording when possible. Turn the microphone on before recording if you need your own narration.

If you need just one passage

Save the extracted audio first. Then use the audio trim tool on a copy. Leave a short margin before the first word and after the last one, then listen to both cuts. Space Sheep Studio develops this iPhone app. Export tools require Pro.
