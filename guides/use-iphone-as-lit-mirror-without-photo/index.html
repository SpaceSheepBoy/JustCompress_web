Use Your iPhone as a Lit Mirror Without a Photo

Open a live front-camera mirror with adjustable screen light. Set the light while you check your face, and leave the capture button untouched if you only need a preview.

Quick check

Use the screen for a quick check

A front camera helps you check a small area when a mirror or lamp is out of reach. Camera exposure and your screen can change the appearance, so use the view to check edges and placement.

Try it on iPhone

  1. Open GlowPop: Selfie Glow Camera and grant camera access. Put the phone at a comfortable distance where you can see the live front-camera view.
  2. Begin with a neutral screen color and increase brightness in small steps. Tilt the phone slightly if you see glare. Check the area you want without tapping capture.
  3. Check the result in ordinary room light or a physical mirror when color or texture matters. Close the preview when you are finished.

Keep your hands free

Rest the phone on a stand if you need both hands. Move the phone or turn your face for a better angle. If the light feels uncomfortable or you need to examine a precise shade, switch to a mirror in soft light.

Preview and capture are separate

Space Sheep Studio develops GlowPop for iPhone. The current listing lets you adjust the screen light while the camera is open and preview every light color for free.
