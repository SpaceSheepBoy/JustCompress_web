Combine Portrait and Landscape Photos Without Cropping

Put the photos in one row or column and fit each image inside its space. Leave extra space around any image that cannot fill its space without losing edges.

Quick check

Make space for both shapes

A portrait image is tall and a landscape image is wide. When both go into equal boxes, an edge-to-edge layout can cut one of them. Extra space lets more of the original image remain visible.

Try it on iPhone

  1. Select the two images in Combine Photos: Stitch & Grid and preview a row or a vertical layout.
  2. Move each photo within its space and zoom out enough to show the important edges. If a cell still crops an edge, try a different layout before exporting.
  3. Save a JPEG or PNG. Reopen the saved image and check the corners of each photo at the size the recipient will see.

Pick the arrangement that keeps details large

In a wide row, the photos share the screen width. On a phone, a column may make each one easier to examine. Compare the saved results if the smallest detail matters, such as lettering at the edge of a sign.

Keep separate files if the recipient needs full resolution

The app exports the combined image up to 3000 pixels wide. Keep the source files if the recipient needs all the original pixels. You can send the combined image as an overview and include individual originals separately.

Common questions

What if the layout crops a corner?

Try a different layout and use the zoom and position controls before you save.

Can I get a print with zero margins and zero cropping?

That depends on the shape of the paper and the images. Use space around the photo if the shapes differ and every edge must remain visible.
