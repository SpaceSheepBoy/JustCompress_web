Convert a Live Photo to SDR Without Choosing a Still Copy

To make a normal SDR Live Photo copy while retaining movement, choose an SDR export that preserves Live Photo motion rather than taking a screenshot. A screenshot records one still frame and cannot carry the moving part or sound.

Choose the approach

Choose what you want to keep

A Live Photo includes a still image and a motion component. HDR appearance and motion are separate questions. Turning off HDR viewing changes playback on that device; it does not by itself create an SDR sharing copy. Keep the original available.

Try it on iPhone

  1. In PhotoFix: HDR to Normal Photos, select the Live Photo and choose normal SDR conversion.
  2. Use the option to retain Live Photo motion and sound for that SDR export. Choose a still copy only when that is the intended deliverable.
  3. Open the saved result in Photos. Check its still appearance, then play the motion and listen to the sound before sharing.

Check the destination too

The receiving app may send only a still image even when the saved item is a Live Photo. Test the actual sharing route with one item before processing a collection. This is not a promise of Live Photo playback on every device or website.

Distinguish the export choices

Space Sheep Studio develops PhotoFix for iPhone. Its current listing supports keeping Live Photo motion for normal SDR export; HDR export creates a still photo. Normal-to-HDR is a creative brightness effect and cannot recover missing detail. The first three successful photo conversions are free, counting each batch item separately.
