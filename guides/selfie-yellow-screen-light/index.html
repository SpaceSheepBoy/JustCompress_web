Selfie Looks Yellow? Check the Room Light and Screen Color

If a selfie looks yellow, start with neutral screen light and reduce competing warm room light, then check a saved photo. Screen color, room lighting and camera white balance all influence the result.

Choose the approach

Change one light source at a time

A nearby warm lamp can tint skin even when the phone screen looks white. Move toward softer neutral light or switch off an unnecessary competing lamp when practical. Keep the phone and face in a similar position while testing so the comparison is meaningful.

Try it on iPhone

  1. Open GlowPop: Selfie Glow Camera and choose a neutral screen color with comfortable brightness.
  2. Check whether a room lamp or colored wall is adding a cast. Adjust that source before cycling through strongly colored screen options.
  3. Take a photo and inspect the saved result. If needed, make one small light-color change and capture another test.

Avoid chasing a perfect preview

The camera may adapt its exposure or white balance between frames. A white screen is a starting point, not a calibrated daylight source, and GlowPop is not claimed to provide manual Kelvin white balance or a color-calibration tool. Do not judge skin color from a brightly tinted screen alone.
