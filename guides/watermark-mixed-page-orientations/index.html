Watermarking Mixed PDF Pages? Check Portrait and Landscape

When a PDF mixes portrait and landscape pages, inspect the watermark on both shapes before sharing. A mark that fits one page can cover important text or appear differently on another.

Choose the approach

Start with the decision

Page shape and page rotation are related but different. A PDF reader can display a rotated page upright while its internal coordinates differ. Do not assume a preview of the first page proves every later stamp is correctly placed.

Try it on iPhone

  1. Keep the original. Open a copy in PDF Watermark & Stamp, enter the intended wording, and choose the supported repeated diagonal or centered style.
  2. Review a portrait page, a landscape page and any scanned or unusually sized page. Adjust available text size and opacity to keep tables, headings and signatures readable.
  3. Export a new PDF, reopen it outside the app and check those pages again. Confirm that the mark is present, readable and oriented usefully in the file you will send.

Check direction as well as presence

A watermark can exist on every page yet be difficult to read or badly placed on a wide table. Adobe’s watermark documentation treats scale, rotation and position as distinct appearance choices. That is a general PDF principle, not a claim that our app offers every Acrobat control or independent settings for each page.

If the saved placement is wrong

Return to the original and try a shorter phrase or a different supported watermark style. If a page still exports incorrectly, preserve the original and the affected copy for support; do not send an unchecked document. Space Sheep Studio develops this on-device iPhone app. It supports per-page previews and separate marked exports, but this article promises no repair of malformed rotation metadata. A visible watermark is not redaction or copy protection.
