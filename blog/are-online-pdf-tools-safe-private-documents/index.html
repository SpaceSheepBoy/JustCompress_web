Steps, answers & details

A cloud PDF tool may be well run, but it still has to receive your document before it can process it.

  1. Private document Contracts, IDs, bank statements, or medical forms: use a no-upload tool like Compress PDF so the file never leaves your device.
  2. Public + simple A flyer or marketing PDF that isn't sensitive: a reputable cloud tool is perfectly fine.
  3. Needs OCR or editing Scanned text, redaction, or deep edits: use a specialized editor with the privacy controls your case needs.
