Can I split audio into a specific number of parts?

Yes. Choose 2, 3, 4, 5, or 10 parts. Just Compress cuts the recording into equal pieces.

Is this the same as compressing audio?

No. Compression makes one file smaller. Splitting breaks one long file into several parts.

Can I quickly compress audio here?

Yes. Use Compress Audio to shrink one audio file without uploading it.
