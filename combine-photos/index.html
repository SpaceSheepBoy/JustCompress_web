    ❓ Quick answers

    A few useful details

    How do I combine photos into one image?

    Add images, pick Grid, Vertical or Side by side, then download. Use the arrows to reorder images. Each image fits inside a square cell without cropping; portrait images may have extra space around them.

    Is this free, and are my photos uploaded?

    The browser tool is free, with no account or watermark. Photos are processed locally and are not uploaded. The separate iPhone app has its own free allowance and in-app purchases.

    What are the limits?

    Up to 12 JPG, PNG or WebP images, 20 MB each, 80 MB total and 40 megapixels per image. The output is limited to 4096 pixels on its longest side. This basic tool joins images; it does not automatically remove overlapping screenshot content. HEIC and animated formats are not supported.
